Travel Planner helps you search flights, compare options, and organize trip details through natural conversation with Samantha.

You can use it to plan travel, save important details, keep documents together, and stay prepared before and during your trip.

## **What travel planning helps you do**

- **Search flights quickly.** Find options using simple language instead of filters only.
- **Compare choices clearly.** Review prices, timings, airlines, and stops in one place.
- **Refine without restarting.** Adjust dates, time preferences, or budget as you go.
- **Organize the whole trip.** Keep flight details, documents, reminders, and notes together.
- **Prepare better.** Create packing lists and travel reminders before the trip.
- **Find trip info later.** Reopen saved details without digging through messages or files.

## **Search and compare flights**

You can search for flights in natural language.

**For example:**

```text

Find flights from Chennai to Delhi this weekend.

Show the cheapest round trip to Dubai next month.

Look for morning flights to Bangalore on Friday.
```

Samantha can help you compare:

- ticket prices
- airlines
- departure and arrival times
- non-stop and layover options
- return options for round trips

This makes it easier to review choices without jumping across multiple tabs.

## **Refine your options**

You can adjust your search without starting over.

**For example:**

```text

Show evening flights instead.

Try next Thursday.

Only non-stop flights.

Find cheaper options.
```

Samantha updates the results based on what matters most to you, like price, timing, or convenience.

## **Build your trip plan**

Once you choose an option, Samantha can help organize the rest of the trip.

She can help you:

- create a **Trip Details** note
- save flight and booking information
- add travel dates to your calendar
- organize basic itinerary notes
- keep related details in one place

This turns a flight search into a usable trip plan.

## **Store travel documents**

You can keep important travel documents together with the rest of your trip details.

This can include:

- boarding passes
- hotel confirmations
- travel insurance
- visa documents
- booking details

**For example:**

```text

Save this under my Trip Details.

Attach this boarding pass to my trip note.
```

This makes it easier to find everything later when you need it.

## **Packing lists and reminders**

Samantha can also help you prepare for the trip .

You can ask her to:

- create a packing list
- add sections like clothes, toiletries, tech, and essentials
- remind you to check in
- remind you before leaving for the airport
- set hotel checkout reminders

**For example:**

```text

Create a packing list for my trip.

Remind me to check in tomorrow at 10.

Alert me two hours before airport departure.
```

This helps keep travel prep connected to the trip itself.

## **Retrieve trip details anytime**

You can reopen saved trip information whenever you need it.

**For example:**

```text

Open my Trip Details.

Show my return flight.

Find my hotel confirmation.
```

This is useful when your trip is already planned and you just need the information again quickly.

## **Use Maps with your trip**

When location access is enabled, Samantha can also use Maps as part of travel planning.

She can help you:

- estimate travel time
- add locations to your trip plans
- find nearby places
- use location context while planning your schedule

This helps connect your trip details with real-world movement and timing.

## **Offline Behavior**

- Saved notes, to-dos, and trip details are available offline.
- Flight search, pricing, and maps require internet access.
- Data syncs automatically when you reconnect.
