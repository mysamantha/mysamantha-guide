Everything you capture is saved directly into your **MySamantha Notes**, so your ideas, research, and recordings stay organized in one place.

## **What the browser extension helps you do**

- **Record meetings.** Capture microphone and screen audio from your browser.
- **Clip webpages.** Save full pages or only the parts you want.
- **Collect research across tabs.** Clip from multiple tabs into one note.
- **Take quick notes while browsing.** Write things down without switching apps.
- **Capture screenshots instantly.** Save visual context while you work.
- **Keep everything together.** Notes, clips, screenshots, and recordings all stay in MySamantha.

## Meeting Capture

Use Meeting Capture when you want to record a call, lecture, interview, or demo from your browser.

### It can capture:

- microphone audio
- screen audio
- screenshots during the session.

After recording, you can upload the capture for transcription or download the file.

### How to use

1. Open the MySamantha extension
2. Go to **Meeting Capture**
3. Start recording
4. Take screenshots anytime during the session
5. Stop recording
6. Review the duration and file size
7. Upload for transcription or download the recording

### Use cases

- Online meetings
- Lectures
- Interviews
- Product demos
- Discussions you want to revisit later

<Note>
   Recordings, transcriptions, and screenshots stay together in a single note, so nothing gets lost or scattered.
</Note>

## Web Clipper (Full Page \+ Selected Elements)

<video src="/videos/clipper(1)(1)(1)-1.mp4" controls />

Use Web Clipper when you want to save information from a webpage directly into Notes.

### You can save:

- a full webpage
- selected elements from a page

**Save a full page**

1. Open the extension
2. Click **Save Full Page \> Clipper**
3. Preview the content
4. Save as a new note
5. Edit the note title if needed

**Clip selected elements**

1. Click **Start Clipping**
2. Select elements on the page
3. Save to a new note or an existing one
4. Edit the note title before saving

### Use cases

- Saving articles and documentation
- Collecting highlights and references
- Storing long-form research material

<Note>
  You can capture both full pages and specific content using one tool, without copying, pasting, or bookmarking manually.
</Note>

## Multi-Tab Clipping

Use Multi-Tab Clipping when your research is spread across several browser tabs.

It lets you collect content from multiple webpages and save everything into one note.

### How to enable

1. Go to **Profile → Settings → Web Clipper**
2. Enable **Multi-Tab Clipping**

### How it works

1. Start clipping on one webpage
2. Switch to another browser tab
3. Continue selecting elements
4. Save all selected content together into one note

### Use cases

- Research across multiple websites
- Comparisons
- Grouped references and resources

<Note>
  Related content from different sources stays organized in one place instead of being split across multiple notes.
</Note>

The extension also lets you create notes directly while browsing.

You can:

- write a quick note
- add screenshots
- save everything without leaving the page you are on

### How to use

1. Open the **Notes** tab in the extension
2. Write your note
3. Add screenshots
4. Save

### Use cases

- Quick ideas while browsing
- Visual references
- Contextual notes tied to webpages

<Note>
  You capture thoughts and visuals without switching apps or breaking your flow.
</Note>

## Where Everything Is Saved

Everything captured from the extension is saved into **MySamantha Notes**.

That includes:

- meeting recordings
- transcriptions
- webpage clips
- screenshots
- notes created in the extension

These notes work just like any other note in MySamantha. You can edit, label, pin, move, or delete them anytime.

## **Privacy and control**

- Everything you save stays in your MySamantha workspace
- You stay in control of what gets captured and saved
- Saved content can be edited or deleted later
