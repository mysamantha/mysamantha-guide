Samantha is your in-app assistant in MySamantha. She helps you manage notes, tasks, journals, email, calendar, and more from one connected workspace.

You can ask Samantha to capture information, find what matters, turn conversations into actions, and help you follow through. Samantha only acts when you ask.

## **Talking to Samantha**

You can interact with Samantha in natural language.

You can:

- Type messages
- Use voice input
- Continue conversations over time

You don't need special commands or formats.

## **What Samantha Can Do**

Samantha helps you manage work and life from one place.

**She can:**

- Create notes, to dos, and journal entries
- Edit or summarize existing content
- Turn conversations into actions
- Retrieve past notes, tasks, or entries
- Coordinate actions across email, calendar, and maps (when connected)

## **Conversations to Actions**

Turns chat messages into structured actions.

**For Example:**

- A thought can become a note
- A plan can become a task list
- A follow-up can become a reminder
- A date you mention can become a calendar event
- A rough message can become a draft email

## **Memory & Context**

\
Maintains context across conversations.

**Samantha can remember:**

- Previous chats
- Related notes and tasks
- Past decisions and references

This allows follow-ups without repeating yourself.

## **Smart Recall**

\
Finds relevant past information when needed.

**How it works**

- Samantha searches your notes, journals, tasks, and chats
- Relevant context is surfaced automatically

This helps maintain continuity over time.

## **Integrations Support**

When integrations are connected, Samantha can work across your tools from the same conversation. This helps you move from information to action without switching context.

### **Samantha can help with:**

- **Email**\
  Summarize emails, draft replies, find follow-ups, and turn messages into tasks or events
- **Calendar**\
  Review your schedule, create events, and help plan around upcoming meetings
- **Google Maps**\
  Use location context for planning, places, and travel-related coordination
- **Slack**\
  Send messages, summarize threads, find updates, and turn conversations into notes or tasks
- **HubSpot**\
  Find contacts, review deals, update records, add notes, and support follow-up workflows
- **Eventbrite**\
  Review events, check registrations, summarize event details, and organize follow-up work
- **Linear**\
  Find issues, create tickets, review status, and turn discussions into actionable work
- **Notion**\
  Find pages, summarize docs, create content, and pull action items from workspace content
- **GitHub**\
  Review repositories, find issues or pull requests, create issues, and summarize project activity
- **Cloudflare**\
  Review domains, inspect DNS records, summarize setup details, and support website operations

All integrations are optional and permission-based.

## **Writing and Drafting**

\
Helps write content with review control.

**Samantha can:**

- Draft emails
- Rewrite or refine text
- Generate summaries or explanations

Nothing is sent or saved without your approval.

## **Image Generation**

Create images inside Samantha using natural language. Describe what you want, and Samantha can generate visuals to support ideas, content, and creative work.

**What image generation helps you do**

- Generate images from natural language prompts
- Explore visual ideas quickly
- Create simple visuals for content, planning, or creative work
- Refine prompts and try different directions in chat

**How it works**

Ask Samantha for the image you want to create. You can describe the subject, style, mood, or use case in plain language. Samantha will generate images based on your prompt.

<Accordion title="Example prompts">
  Create an image of a calm modern workspace

  Generate a simple hero image for a landing page

  Make an illustration for a product update post

  Create 3 visual directions for a homepage concept
</Accordion>

**Review and control**

Image generation happens only when you ask. You stay in control of the prompt and can refine it based on the result.

**How it works**

- Every file you send in chat is saved automatically
- You can browse, search, and reopen any file anytime
- Files are organized and accessible from the Files section in the assistant

## **Share Chat**

Share a conversation when someone else needs the context, decisions, or next steps.

- Create a share link for a chat
- Choose whether it stays auto-updated as the conversation continues
- Choose whether to include attachments

**Links and limits**

- Each chat can have up to 5 active share links at a time
- Delete a link anytime to free up a slot

**Privacy and control**

- Sharing is always optional and permission-based
- Nothing is shared without your approval
- Share links can be deleted at any time

## **Offline Behavior**

When offline:

- Notes, to-do, and journals still work
- Samantha chat and AI actions are unavailable

Everything syncs once you reconnect.

  Samantha helps organize, remember, and act when you ask.
</Note>
