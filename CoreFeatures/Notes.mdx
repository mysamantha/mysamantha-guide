Notes in MySamantha help you capture anything quickly, whether it is a typed thought, a voice note, or a file you want to keep. Once it is saved, you can organize it, ask Samantha to help with it, share it by link or publicly, and turn it into something useful when you need to.

## **Create Notes**

You can create notes in multiple ways:

- Type directly in the Notes section
- Use voice to record and transcribe thoughts
- Ask Samantha to create a note from chat

## **Voice to Note**

Use voice when you want to capture something fast.

**How to use**

1. Tap the microphone
2. Speak naturally
3. Your note is transcribed and saved automatically

## **Use slash commands**

Type **/** inside a note to quickly access formatting, insert options, and note actions without leaving the editor.

## **Labels**

**How to use**

- Create your own labels
- Apply one or more labels to a note
- Search or filter notes using labels

Labels help when you manage multiple projects or topics.

## **Dynamic AI Insights**

AI that acts on any text you highlight

### **What you can do**

- Summarize key points
- Turn content into To-Dos
- Draft emails directly from notes
- Explain or simplify complex text
- Create meeting recaps
- Enhance or polish writing
- Rewrite in different tones or styles
- Generate creative outputs like poems or alternate versions
- Ask anything related to your highlighted content

### **How to use**

1. Highlight text inside a note
2. Type what you want Samantha to do
3. The content transforms instantly

  Dynamic AI Insights require an internet connection.

## Chat with Notes

Ask questions, get insights, and turn your notes into actions , just by chatting.

### What you can do

- Turn meeting notes into follow-up messages
- Identify decisions, action points, or missing details
- Rewrite content in a clearer or more professional tone
- Break plans into step-by-step actions
- Extract deadlines, tasks, or key information
- Simplify complex ideas
- Brainstorm or expand on your existing notes

### How to use

1. Open any note.
2. Tap the Chat with Samantha in the top-right corner.
3. Start chatting with Samantha.
4. Ask anything - Samantha understands your note's context automatically.

## **Pin Notes**

**How to use**

- Pin a note
- It stays visible above others in its category

Pinned notes are useful for ongoing or critical information.

## **Attachments**

Attach files directly to a note so everything stays together.

**Supported attachments**

- Images
- Audio recordings
- Documents

Attachments stay linked to the note, so everything related stays together.

## **Export**

You can export notes when you need a copy outside MySamantha.

**Available formats**

- PDF
- HTML

**How to use**

1. Click a note menu (**:**)
2. Choose Export
3. Select the format

## **Public Notes**

\
Lets you publish a note and share it with anyone using a link.

**How to make a note public in MySamantha**

1. Open the note you want to share
2. Tap the <Icon icon="globe" /> icon
3. Choose Share
4. Toggle “Public” or “Anyone with link” ON
5. Tap “Copy link” and send it

**Good for**

- Shared docs and checklists
- Meeting notes and recaps
- Portfolios and templates

<Warning>
  Avoid making notes public if they contain passwords, OTPs, ID numbers, bank details, or anything private or personal.
</Warning>

**What it does**\
Brings existing Notion notes into [mysamantha.ai](http://mysamantha.ai).

**How to use**

## **Using Notes with Samantha**

You can manage notes directly from chat.

**Examples**

- "Create a note from this."
- "Summarize this note."
- "Turn this into a to-do."
- "Open my meeting notes."
- "Add this to note. "

Samantha only acts when you ask.

## **Offline Behavior**

When offline, you can:

- Create new notes
- Edit existing notes
- Organize, pin, and label notes

Dynamic Insights and assistant features resume once you're back online.

## **Privacy & Control**

- Notes are encrypted
- Your data is never sold
- Notes are not used to train AI models
- You can export or delete notes anytime. [more](https://mysamantha.ai/privacy)
