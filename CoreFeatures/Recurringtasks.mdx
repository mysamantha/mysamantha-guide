Set tasks to repeat automatically so you do not have to recreate them every day, week, month, or year.

Recurring tasks are useful for routines like daily medication, weekly reviews, monthly bills, or annual deadlines. Set the pattern once, and MySamantha keeps the schedule going for you.

## What are recurring tasks?

Recurring tasks help you automate work that happens on a regular schedule.

### Common examples

- Daily medication
- Weekly team meetings
- Monthly rent
- Quarterly reviews
- Yearly tax filing

### How it works

When you complete one recurring task, the next occurrence is created automatically based on the pattern you set.

## How to create recurring tasks

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile app">
    1. Create a new task
    2. Click the **Date** field
    3. Open the **Recurring** tab
    4. Choose the **start date**
    5. Set the **start time** and **end time**
    6. Choose how often the task should repeat:
       - Every day
       - Every week
       - Every month
       - Every year
       - **Custom**
    7. Save the task
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web app">
    1. Create a new task
    2. Under **Task Type**, choose **Recurring**
    3. Open **Frequency**
    4. Choose the repeat pattern:
       - Every day
       - Every week
       - Every month
       - Every year
       - **Custom**
    5. Set **Start time** and **Due time** under **Instance Times** if needed
    6. Choose the **List**
    7. Click **Create task**
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Use **Custom** when you want a more specific repeat pattern.

## Recurring task patterns

### Daily patterns

- **Every day**
- **Every weekday**
- **Every 2 days**
- **Every 3 days**

### Weekly patterns

- **Every week**
- **Every Monday**
- **Every Monday and Friday**
- **Every weekend**
- **Every other week**
- **Every 3 weeks**
- **Every Monday, Wednesday, Friday**
- **Every Tuesday, Thursday**

### Monthly patterns

- **Every month**
- **Every 15th**
- **Every 2 months**
- **Every 3 months**
- **Every 6 months**

### Yearly patterns

- **Every year**
- **Every January 15th**
- **Every December 25th**

### Time-based recurring tasks

You can add a time to any recurring task.

- **Every Monday at 9 AM**
- **Every first Friday at 10:30 AM**
- **Every weekday at 8:30 AM**
- **Every 2 weeks at 3 PM**

### Ending on a specific date

- **Every day until March 31**
- **Every Monday ending December 15**
- **Every week for 3 months**

## Limitations and workarounds

Some repeat patterns are not supported yet. Here’s the easiest workaround for each one.

### Different times for different days

**Not supported:** Every Monday at 8 PM and Tuesday at 9 PM

**Workaround:** Create two separate recurring tasks

### Multiple recurrence rules in one task

**Not supported:** Every Monday and every 1st of the month

**Workaround:** Create two separate recurring tasks

## Managing recurring tasks

### Completing a recurring task

Each recurring task creates the next scheduled task automatically.

If you do not complete today’s task, the next one will still appear on the scheduled date. Uncompleted tasks are marked as overdue.

### Editing a recurring task

Changes apply to all future occurrences.

If you change the frequency or interval, the recurrence pattern recalculates from today.

### Ending a recurring task

You can choose when the task stops repeating:

- **Never**
- **On date**
- **After several occurrences**

### Deleting a recurring task

Deleting a recurring task removes all future occurrences.

If you only want to remove one occurrence, complete it instead or convert it to a one-time task first.

You can also open the **menu (⋮)** and select **Show all recurring instances** to see all upcoming occurrences of a recurring task in one place.
