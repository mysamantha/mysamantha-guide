To Do in MySamantha helps you keep track of what needs to happen next. Add tasks in seconds, break bigger work into smaller steps, build recurring routines, and ask Samantha to help you plan your day around what matters most.

<video src="/videos/tasknew.mp4" controls />

## What you can do with To Do

- **Create tasks instantly.** Type them in or tell Samantha what needs doing and she’ll add them for you.
- **Set due dates and reminders.** Keep track of deadlines and make sure nothing gets missed.
- **Break work into subtasks.** Turn bigger goals into smaller, manageable steps.
- **Organize with sections.** Group tasks by project, priority, or part of your day.
- **Build recurring tasks.** Set up daily, weekly, or monthly routines once and let them repeat automatically.
- **Prioritize what matters.** Mark important tasks so it’s easier to focus on what needs attention first.
- **Plan your day with Samantha.** Ask her what’s due, what’s overdue, and what to tackle next.

## Who it's for

If your day lives across mental notes, sticky reminders, and things you swear you’ll remember later, To Do helps bring it all into one place. Capture what needs doing, stay organized, and get help from Samantha when it’s time to plan.
