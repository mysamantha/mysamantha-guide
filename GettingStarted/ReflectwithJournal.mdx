Journal in MySamantha is your private space to reflect. It's more than a diary. Samantha helps you track how you're feeling, spot patterns over time, and turn your reflections into something meaningful.

<video src="/videos/journalnew.mp4" controls />

## What you can do with Journal

- **Write freely.** Capture daily entries, quick check-ins, or longer reflections in your own words.
- **Log your mood.** Keep track of how you’re feeling and look back on patterns over time.
- **Get prompts from Samantha.** Start with a question or idea when you’re not sure what to write.
- **Reflect with Samantha.** Ask her to summarize entries, spot patterns, or help you think through what you’ve written.
- **Look back over time.** Revisit past entries and see how your mindset, habits, and emotions have changed.
- **Keep it private.** Your journal stays personal and secure.

## Who it's for

If you ever need a place to clear your head, process what happened, or make sense of how you’ve been feeling, Journal gives you that space. Write when you need to, come back anytime, and let Samantha help you reflect more deeply.
