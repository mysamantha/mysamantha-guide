---
title: "How MySamantha Works"
---

MySamantha brings your notes, tasks, journal, and planning into one workspace. Samantha is the AI assistant across it all, helping you capture ideas, stay organized, and turn thoughts into action.

## **Start Wherever Your Mind Is**

You do not need to begin with a perfect plan. Start with whatever is on your mind.

With MySamantha, you can:

- **Write a note** when you want to capture an idea
- **Add a task** when something needs to get done
- **Journal** when you want to reflect
- **Talk to Samantha** when you want help thinking, planning, or taking action

## **Turn thoughts into action**

As you use MySamantha, ideas can move naturally from one step to the next.

- **Thoughts become notes**
- **Notes can become tasks**
- **Conversations can create reminders**
- **Reflections can reveal patterns over time**

Samantha helps you take the next step when you are ready.

## **Talk naturally with Samantha**

You can speak to Samantha the way you would speak to a person. You do not need special commands or rigid formatting.

You can say things like:

- “Remind me if they reply.”
- “Turn this into a task.”
- “Help me plan my day.”
- “Summarize what I wrote last week.”

Samantha understands context and only takes action when you ask.

## **Works across what you connect**

Samantha can also help with the tools you connect to MySamantha.

- **Email** to review messages, draft replies, and spot follow-ups
- **Calendar** to check your schedule and manage events
- **Location tools** to help with directions, planning, and time-aware suggestions

You stay in control of what is connected.

## **Works online and offline**

You can still use core parts of MySamantha without internet.

- **Offline:** notes, tasks, and journal entries
- **Online:** chat, AI actions, email, calendar, maps, and notifications

Everything syncs when you are back online.
