MySamantha brings your notes, tasks, journal, and planning into one workspace.

Samantha is the assistant across it all, helping you capture ideas, stay organized, and turn thoughts into action.

## Start anywhere

You do not need a perfect system before you begin. Start with whatever is on your mind.

## Talk naturally with Samantha

You can speak to Samantha the way you would speak to a person. No special commands needed.

**Examples:**

```text
Remind me if they reply.

Turn this into a task.

Help me plan my day.

Summarize what I wrote last week.
```

Samantha understands context and only takes action when you ask.
