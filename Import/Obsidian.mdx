---
title: "Obsidian"
---

If you already use Obsidian, you can import your notes into Samantha and continue working with them in one place.

This makes it easier to bring your existing notes, knowledge, and writing into your daily workflow with Samantha.

## Why import from Obsidian

Importing from Obsidian helps you:

- Move your existing notes into Samantha
- Keep important information in one workspace
- connect notes more easily with tasks, planning, and assistant workflows

## What you can import

You can import your existing Obsidian notes and review them inside Samantha after the import is complete.

## Before you import

Before starting, it helps to:

- Clean up old or duplicate notes
- Review your folders and note structure
- Decide which notes you want to bring to Samantha

## How to import from Obsidian

1. Open the import or migration section
2. Choose **Obsidian**
3. Follow the steps shown in the app
4. Wait for the import to finish
5. Review your imported notes

## After import

Once your notes are imported, check that:

- titles look correct
- structure is preserved
- formatting appears as expected

<Note>
  If needed, you can reorganize or clean up notes after import.
</Note>
