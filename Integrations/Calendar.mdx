Connect your calendar so Samantha can help you manage events, plan your schedule, and stay on top of meetings and deadlines.

Once connected, Samantha can understand your schedule, create or move events, and help you plan around what is already on your calendar.

## **What calendar integration helps you do**

- **See your schedule clearly.** Ask what is coming up today, tomorrow, or this week.
- **Create events faster.** Add meetings and plans using natural language.
- **Reschedule easily.** Move events when plans change.
- **Avoid conflicts.** Check your availability before adding something new.
- **Plan around meetings.** Add reminders, prep time, or follow-up tasks around events.
- **Work across tools.** Connect calendar with email, tasks, and planning workflows.

## **How It Works (Web)**

1. Open **MySamantha**
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Select **Google Integration** or **Outlook Integration**
4. Click **Add Account**
5. You’ll be redirected to sign in with your calendar provider
6. Approve access to your calendar
   <video src="/videos/email.mp4" controls />

Once connected, you can ask Samantha to create, move, or review events.

## **How It Works (Mobile App)**

1. Open the **MySamantha**
2. Tap **Settings**
3. Select **Google Integration** or **Outlook Integration**
4. Tap **Add Account**
5. Sign in and approve access

Your calendar syncs automatically after connection.

## **Supported Calendars**

MySamantha syncs with your existing calendar accounts.

- Google Calendar
- Outlook Calendar

You can manage connected calendars anytime from Settings.

## **Work with calendar using Samantha**

Once your calendar is connected, Samantha can help you manage your schedule more naturally.

She can help you:

- Create new events
- Update event titles, dates, times, or details
- Move or reschedule meetings
- Check what is on your calendar
- Find free time in your schedule
- Reference upcoming or past events when you ask

Samantha only takes action when you ask.

## **Scheduling and rescheduling**

You do not need to think in formal calendar steps. You can describe plans the way you normally would.

For example, Samantha can help with things like:

```text

Add a meeting tomorrow at 3 PM called Project Review.

Move my meeting with Alex from Friday to Monday.

Schedule a call next week when I’m free.

Block one hour before the meeting for prep.
```

She can also help detect conflicts before scheduling and suggest a better time when needed.

## **Plan around your day**

Calendar works best when it connects with the rest of your planning.

Samantha can help you:

- Check what is coming up today
- Find time between existing events
- Add reminders around meetings
- Turn meetings into follow-up tasks
- Keep email, calendar, and tasks aligned

This makes your calendar feel like part of your workflow, not a separate tool you have to manage manually.

**Example prompts**

```text

What’s on my calendar tomorrow?

Add a meeting tomorrow at 3 PM called Project Review.

Move my meeting with Alex from Friday to Monday.

When am I free next week?

Add a reminder 30 minutes before this event.

Turn this meeting into follow-up tasks.
```

## **Works with your planning**

When the calendar is connected with your other tools, Samantha can help across them together.

For example, she can help you:

```text

Schedule from an email thread

Check calendar conflicts before confirming a time

Create tasks from a meeting

Add reminders tied to an event

Keep follow-ups connected to the right context
```

This helps your schedule stay connected to what actually needs to get done.

## **Memory & Context**

Samantha remembers:

- Past meetings
- Scheduling preferences
- Previous conversations related to events

This helps her plan better over time instead of starting fresh every time.

## **Offline Behavior**

Calendar actions require an internet connection.

**When offline:**

- You can still view notes, to dos, and journals
- Calendar updates will resume once you’re back online

## **Disconnecting Calendar**

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Calendar**
2. Select the connected account
3. Click **Disconnect**

Once disconnected, Samantha will no longer access your calendar.
