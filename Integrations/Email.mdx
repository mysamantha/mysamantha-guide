## **What email integration helps you do**

- **Review emails faster.** Summarize messages and long threads without reading every line.
- **Draft replies.** Write responses faster and review them before sending.
- **Track follow-ups.** Get reminded when someone replies or when an email needs action.
- **Turn email into action.** Convert messages into tasks, reminders, or calendar events.
- **Stay in context.** Work from the email you are already discussing instead of starting over.
- **Use connected tools together.** Link your email with your calendar, tasks, and planning workflows.

## **Connect your email**

You can connect your email on web or mobile.

### On web 

1. Open **MySamantha**
2. Go to **Settings** 
3. Select **Google Integration** or **Outlook Integration**
4. Click **Add Account**
5. Sign in and approve access

You can connect multiple email accounts.

### **On mobile**

1. Open the **MySamantha** app
2. Tap **Profile**
3. Select **Google Integration** or **Outlook Integration**
4. Tap **Add Account**
5. Sign in and approve access

Your inbox syncs automatically after connection.

## **Supported Email Providers**

MySamantha supports:

- Gmail
- Outlook

You can connect and manage multiple accounts from Settings.

## **Work with email using Samantha**

Once email is connected, Samantha can help you handle messages in a more natural way.

She can help you:

- Summarize an email or thread
- Pull out key decisions or action items
- Draft a reply
- Rewrite a message in a different tone
- Find follow-ups you should not miss
- Help you respond using the context of the conversation

You can ask naturally, without special commands.

## **Turn email into action**

Email is often where work starts. Samantha helps turn it into the next step.

For example, she can help turn:

- An email for a task
- A follow-up as a reminder
- A scheduling thread for a calendar event
- A decision in an email into notes or action items

This is especially useful when email leads to work in other parts of MySamantha.

## **Monitoring and reminders**

Samantha can also help you stay on top of important replies and conditions.

You can ask for things like:

- Notify me when they reply
- Remind me if this client emails back
- Watch for emails about a specific topic
- Let me know if a certain person responds

This helps you track important conversations without checking your inbox constantly.

**Example prompts**

```text

Summarize this email thread.

Draft a reply and confirm the meeting.

Turn this email into tasks.

Remind me if they reply.

Schedule a call next week when I’m free.

What do I need to follow up on here?
```

## **Memory & Context**

Samantha remembers:

- Previous conversations
- Past emails you discussed
- Notes, tasks, and decisions

This allows her to respond with context instead of starting from scratch.

## **Offline Behavior**

Email features require an internet connection.

When offline:

- Notes, to dos, and journals still work
- Email actions resume once you reconnect

## **Privacy & Control**

- Samantha accesses email only for requested actions
- Emails are encrypted
- Your data is never sold or used to train AI models
- You can disconnect email anytime

## **Disconnecting Email**

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Email**
2. Select the connected account
3. Click **Disconnect**

Once disconnected, Samantha will no longer access your inbox.
