With location access enabled, Samantha can help you search nearby places, understand travel time, add locations to plans, and make scheduling decisions with real-world context.

## **What Maps integration helps you do**

- **Find places faster.** Search for nearby restaurants, coffee shops, stores, and other places when you need them.
- **Check useful details.** View ratings and place context before deciding where to go.
- **Estimate travel time.** See how long it may take to get somewhere.
- **Plan around location.** Add places to your plans, meetings, and daily flow.
- **Think in a real-world context.** Use travel time and nearby options when making decisions.
- **Connect location with planning.** Use Maps together with calendar, tasks, and reminders.

## **Enable location access**

The first time you use Samantha for location-aware help, MySamantha asks for permission to access your location.

## **How It Works**

The first time you open the Samantha chat, MySamantha asks for permission to use your location.

You don’t need to go to Settings or trigger a specific command.

1. Open the **Samantha chat**
2. You’ll be asked to grant location permission
3. Tap **Allow** to continue

Once allowed, Samantha can use location context whenever it’s relevant.

## **If location access is off**

If location access is denied, Samantha may ask again the next time a request needs it.

If location access has been blocked, you can enable it manually from your device settings:

1. Open your phone **Settings**
2. Go to **Apps**
3. Select **MySamantha**
4. Open **Permissions**
5. Turn on **Location**

Once enabled again, Samantha can use location context in relevant requests.

## **Work with Maps using Samantha**

With Maps access, Samantha can help you:

```text

Find nearby places

Understand locations mentioned in conversation

Estimate travel time between places

Add location context to your plans

Use travel time when helping with scheduling

Reference saved or recent places when relevant
```

Samantha only uses location data for actions you ask for.

## **Plan around places and time**

You do not need to think in addresses or route steps. You can describe plans naturally.

For example, Samantha can help with requests like:

```text

Find coffee shops near my next meeting.

How long will it take to get to the airport from home?

Add this location to my meeting.

Plan my route for this evening.

Restaurants near me.

Remind me when I’m close to the store.
```

This makes Maps useful not just for search, but for real planning.

## **Works with your planning**

Maps becomes more useful when it works with the rest of your workspace.

Samantha can help you:

```text

Add locations to calendar events

Think about travel time before meetings

Reference places mentioned in notes or emails

Connect tasks to real places

Keep plans grounded in where things actually happen
```

This helps your planning feel more connected to the real world.

## **Offline Behavior**

Location-based features require an internet connection.

When offline:

- Notes, to dos, and journals still work
- Location features resume once you reconnect

## **Privacy & Control**

- You’re asked for location permission when you enter the chat
- If denied repeatedly, you control access from device settings
- Location data is used only for actions you request
- Your data is encrypted
- Your data is never sold or used to train AI models
- You can revoke access anytime from your device settings
