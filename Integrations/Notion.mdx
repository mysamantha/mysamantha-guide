Connect Notion so Samantha can help you work across docs, databases, and workspace knowledge without switching context.

**What Notion integration helps you do**

- Find pages or databases
- Pull information from Notion pages
- Summarize long docs
- Create pages or add content
- Organize research, plans, and meeting notes
- Turn Notion content into tasks, notes, or follow-ups
- Draft cleaner docs from rough ideas
- Keep Notion connected with your other workflows

**Connect Notion**

1. Open **MySamantha**

2. Go to **Settings**

3. Select **Integrations**

4. Choose **Notion**

5. Sign in and approve access

**Work with Notion using Samantha**  

Once connected, you can ask Samantha to find pages, summarize documents, create content, pull action items, clean up rough notes, and connect what is in Notion with your tasks, notes, and planning workflows.

<Accordion title="Example prompts">
  Find my homepage planning doc in Notion

  Summarize this Notion page

  Create a Notion page for homepage testimonials

  Pull action items from this Notion doc

  Turn this note into a cleaner spec

  Find my product roadmap in Notion
</Accordion>
