Import your existing Notion content into MySamantha so you can keep your notes and start working without rebuilding everything from scratch.

Imported Notion pages are brought into MySamantha as notes, so your existing knowledge stays usable inside the same workspace where you manage notes, tasks, and planning.

## **What Notion import helps you do**

- **Move notes faster.** Bring existing content into MySamantha without manual copy-paste.
- **Keep your knowledge usable.** Continue working with what you already wrote in Notion.
- **Start organizing right away.** Imported content behaves like regular notes in MySamantha.
- **Use Samantha on old content.** Summarize, search, rewrite, or turn imported notes into tasks.
- **Avoid starting over.** Keep your existing information instead of rebuilding your system from scratch.

## **What Gets Imported**

When you import from Notion:

- Text content is preserved
- Headings and structure are maintained
- Notes are created inside **Notes**

## **How to Import from Notion**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In mobile app">
    1. Open Notes
    2. Click the menu
    3. Select **Import from Notion**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In web app">
    1. Go to **Profile**.
    2. Open **Settings**.
    3. Navigate to **Imports**.
    4. Choose **Notion** and **Authorize Access**.
    5. Select the content you want to import.
       <Frame>
         ![Notion](/images/notion.png)
       </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## **After Import**

Once imported:

- Each Notion page becomes a note
- Titles are generated from page names
- You can edit, label, pin, or organize notes normally

Imported notes behave the same as any other note.

## **Using Imported Notes with Samantha**

After import, you can ask Samantha to:

- Summarize imported notes
- Turn notes into to dos
- Explain or rewrite content
- Find information from old notes

This helps make existing content actionable.

## **Offline Behavior**

- Import requires an internet connection
- Imported notes are available offline once synced

## **Privacy & Control**

- Data is encrypted
- Imported content is not sold or used to train AI models
- You can delete imported notes anytime
