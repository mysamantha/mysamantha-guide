---

Email Footer lets you define the sign-off Samantha adds to your drafted emails.

This helps keep your messages consistent and ready to send without rewriting the same closing every time.

## **What you can include**

Your footer can include:

- your name
- your role
- your company
- contact details
- a preferred sign-off

## Why use an email footer

A saved footer helps your drafts feel more polished and consistent.

It also saves time when you regularly send similar emails.

## How it works

When Samantha drafts an email for you, it can include your saved footer automatically.

You can update it anytime if your title, contact details, or preferred closing changes.

## How to update your email footer

1. Open **Settings**
2. Select **Email Footer**
3. Add or edit your footer text
4. Save your changes

<Tip>
  - Keep your footer short and clear
  - Use a version that works for most emails
  - Update it when your role or contact details change
</Tip>
