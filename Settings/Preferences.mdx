Preferences lets you choose how Samantha behaves across different parts of your workspace.

Use this page to adjust defaults for transcription, task handling, and email notifications so Samantha works the way you prefer.

## What you can manage

Preferences are grouped by feature area:

- Notes
- Transcription
- Todos
- Email

## Notes

### Speaker labels

Turn on **Speaker labels** if you want transcripts to separate each speaker in a conversation.

This is useful when you're working with calls, meetings, or interviews where more than one person is speaking.

## Transcription

### Use long transcription key for short transcription too

Turn on this setting if you want short audio to also use your long transcription provider setup.

When enabled, short audio is routed through your Deepgram key instead of Groq.

## Todos

### Auto archive completed tasks

Turn on **Auto archive completed tasks** if you want completed tasks to be removed from your active view automatically.

This helps keep your task list clean once work is finished.

### Auto archive cancelled tasks

Turn on **Auto archive cancelled tasks** if you want cancelled tasks to be archived automatically.

This is useful if you want to keep your active task list focused only on work that is still in progress.

### Show all recurring instances

Turn on **Show all recurring instances** if you want to display every upcoming occurrence of recurring tasks.

This makes it easier to see the full schedule of repeating tasks ahead of time.

## Email

### Notify me about email replies

Turn on **Notify me about email replies** if you want Samantha to send you a chat message when an email you sent gets a reply or bounces.

This helps you stay on top of important follow-ups without checking manually.

## How to update preferences

1. Open **Settings**
2. Select **Preferences**
3. Turn each option on or off
4. Changes apply automatically to the related feature
