---
title: "Backup and Sync"
---

Use **Backup & Sync** in Settings to connect external services for backup and import workflows.

Go to **Settings → Backup & Sync** to connect supported services.

### **Supported services**

#### Notion

Back up notes to your Notion workspace.

#### Todoist

Back up todo lists to Todoist projects.

#### Google Drive

Import documents from Google Drive into your notes.

Google Drive also supports **Daily backup** for notes. When enabled, Samantha saves a copy of every note to a **“Samantha Notes Backup”** folder in your Google Drive as **Google Docs**, with images embedded and audio recordings linked. Backups run daily and only update notes that have changed.

### **How to enable a backup**

1. Open **Settings**
2. Select **Backup & Sync**
3. Click **Connect** next to the service
4. Complete authorization in the popup
5. For Google Drive, turn on **Daily backup** if you want automatic note backups

### **What this is useful for**

- Keeping note backups in Notion or Google Drive
- Importing Drive documents into notes
- Syncing todo lists with Todoist
- Maintaining an updated external copy of changed notes
