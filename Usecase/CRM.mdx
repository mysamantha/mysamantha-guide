# Client CRM

Manage clients, conversations, and follow-ups in one place so Samantha can help you keep relationship context organized and actionable.

## Best For

- Freelancers and consultants managing clients
- Founders tracking leads and follow-ups
- Anyone who wants a lightweight CRM without a separate tool

## What You’ll Get

- A simple record for each client or lead
- Conversation history in one place
- Follow-up tasks connected to contacts
- Quick summaries before meetings
- Better visibility into next steps

## How client CRM works

### Step 1: Start your tracker

Create a simple system for clients, leads, and follow-ups.

For example:

```text
Create a client CRM for my active clients.

Set up a simple client relationship tracker.

Start a CRM workspace with contacts, notes, and follow-ups.
```

This gives you one place to keep relationship details and updates.

### Step 2: Add contacts and conversation notes

Store important interactions as they happen.

For example:

```text
Create a client record for Acme Labs.

Add today’s client call notes to Rahul’s record.

Save this update under my CRM.
```

This helps you avoid losing important context across meetings, chats, and emails.

### Step 3: Capture follow-ups

Turn conversations into clear next steps.

For example:

```text
Create a follow-up task for this client.

What are the next steps from this call?

Add a reminder to check in next week.
```

This keeps relationships moving instead of getting stuck in notes.

### Step 4: Review recent updates

Ask Samantha for a quick brief before your next touchpoint.

For example:

```text
Summarize recent updates for Acme Labs.

What happened in my last few client conversations?

Give me a quick brief before my next follow-up.
```

This makes it easier to jump back into context fast.

### Step 5: Stay on top of pending work

Track open loops and overdue follow-ups.

For example:

```text
What client follow-ups are pending this week?

Show me overdue follow-ups.

Who do I need to reply to today?
```

This helps you stay consistent and responsive.

Use one note per client or company for cleaner context.\
Keep follow-up tasks tied to the same record.\
Review pending follow-ups at the start of each week.
