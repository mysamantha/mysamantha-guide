Track meals and calorie intake in one place so Samantha can help you stay aware of what you eat and review your habits over time.

## **Best For**

- Anyone working on nutrition goals
- People who want a simple meal log
- Anyone who wants a lightweight calorie tracker

## **What You’ll Get**

- A daily meal log
- Better visibility into calorie intake
- Meal summaries across the day
- Weekly food patterns
- One place to track meals consistently

## **How calorie tracking works**

### **Step 1: Start your tracker**

Create a note or log for the day or week.

**For example:**

```text
Create a calorie tracker for this week with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.

Start today’s meal and calorie log.

Set up a daily food tracker for me.
```

This gives you one place to log meals and stay consistent.

### **Step 2: Log meals and snacks**

Add food entries throughout the day.

**For example:**

```text
Log breakfast: 2 eggs, toast, and coffee.

Add lunch to today’s calorie tracker.

Log an evening snack: banana and peanut butter.
```

This keeps your food log simple and up to date.

### **Step 3: Keep it structured**

Ask Samantha to organize your meals clearly.

**For example:**

```text
Organize today’s meals in my calorie tracker.

Separate my meals into breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.

Clean up my food log for today.
```

This makes your tracker easier to review later.

### **Step 4: Review your day**

Check what you’ve logged so far.

**For example:**

```text
How many calories have I logged today?

Summarize my meals for today.

What have I eaten so far today?
```

This helps you stay aware before the day is over.

### **Step 5: Review patterns over time**

Look back across the week.

**For example:**

```text
Summarize my eating habits this week.

Did I skip any meals this week?

Show me patterns in my calorie tracker.
```

This helps you notice habits, not just totals.

Log meals right after eating for better accuracy.\
Use one tracker per week if you want easier review.\
Review your food patterns regularly to stay consistent.
