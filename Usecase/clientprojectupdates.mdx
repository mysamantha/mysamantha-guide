Status updates eat time. Samantha pulls together what happened, what's next, and what's blocked — so you can send a clean update in minutes.

## Best For

- Professionals managing multiple clients or projects at once
- Anyone who dreads writing weekly status emails
- Teams that need consistent, clear communication with stakeholders

## What You'll Get

- Status updates drafted from your notes, tasks, and emails
- A clear format: done, in progress, blocked, next steps
- Follow-up tasks created from client feedback
- Reminders to send updates on a regular cadence

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Share the context**
> "Draft a weekly update for the Acme project. Here's what happened this week: [paste notes or bullet points]."

**Step 2: Samantha drafts the update**
- Structures it into done, in progress, and next steps
- Keeps the tone professional and concise
- Flags anything that needs client input or approval

**Step 3: Review and send**
> "Make it shorter — 5 bullets max."
> "Add a note that we need their feedback on the design by Friday."

## Example Prompts

- "Draft a client update email for the Acme project based on this week's progress."
- "Summarize the last 7 days on the IndigoTech project in 5 bullets."
- "What projects haven't had an update sent in the last week?"
- "Create a reminder to send status updates every Friday at 4pm."
- "Draft a message to the client flagging a delay in the timeline."
- "Pull all open action items for the Acme account."
- "Write a project kickoff summary to share with the client."
- "What's blocked across my active projects right now?"

## Tips

- Keep a running note per project — dump updates as they happen, not all at once
- Set a weekly reminder to send updates before clients have to ask
- Ask Samantha to flag projects with no recent activity
