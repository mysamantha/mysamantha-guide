Consistent content is hard when you're busy with everything else. Samantha helps you plan ahead, batch ideas, and never miss a publishing date.

## Best For

- Founders, marketers, and creators managing content alongside other work
- Anyone who posts inconsistently because planning feels like too much effort
- Teams that need a shared content schedule that actually gets followed

## What You'll Get

- A weekly or monthly content plan built around your goals
- Ideas generated from your existing work, notes, and conversations
- Reminders to draft, review, and publish on time
- A running backlog of content ideas you can pull from anytime

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Set the context**

> "Help me plan content for next week. I post on LinkedIn and Twitter, 3x per week. My audience is busy professionals."

**Step 2: Samantha builds the plan**

- Suggests topics based on your work, industry, and past content
- Spreads posts across the week with variety in format and tone
- Creates a simple calendar you can follow

**Step 3: Draft and schedule**

> "Draft the LinkedIn post for Monday based on the daily planning topic." "Remind me to review and schedule all posts every Sunday evening."

<Accordion title="Example Prompts">
  Plan my content for next week:  3 LinkedIn posts and 2 tweets

  Generate 10 content ideas based on what I work on

  Draft a LinkedIn post about how I use Samantha for daily planning

  What content am I supposed to post this week?

  Remind me to write my weekly newsletter every Wednesday morning.

  Turn my meeting notes from today into a LinkedIn post.

  Give me a month of content ideas for a productivity-focused audience.

  What topics have I not covered in a while that I should revisit?
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Batch your content planning once a week instead of deciding day by day

  Turn things you already did (meetings, decisions, wins) into content

  Keep a running idea list, and  tell Samantha whenever something comes to mind
</Tip>
