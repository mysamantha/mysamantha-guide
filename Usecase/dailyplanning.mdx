Turn your scattered tasks, calendar, and emails into a focused daily plan before your first meeting.

## Best For

- Busy professionals, founders, and managers
- Anyone who starts the day unsure of what to tackle first
- People juggling meetings, tasks, and emails across multiple tools

## What You'll Get

- A prioritised list of tasks for the day
- A clear view of your meetings and free time blocks
- Suggestions on what to defer, delegate, or drop
- A morning brief you can act on immediately
- Evening wrap-up to close out the day cleanly

## Inputs Samantha Needs

- Access to your Calendar
- Your top 1–3 priorities for the day (optional but helpful)
- Any deadlines or blockers to be aware of
- Your preferred work hours (e.g. 9am–6pm)

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Ask for your daily brief**

> "Give me my plan for today. Summarise my meetings, flag any conflicts, and list my top tasks by priority."

**Step 2: Samantha builds your plan**

- Pulls today's calendar events
- Lists pending tasks sorted by urgency and deadline
- Flags scheduling conflicts or back-to-back meetings
- Suggests time blocks for deep work

**Step 3: You confirm and adjust**

> "Move the 3pm meeting prep to 11am." "Add a task: send proposal to client by 4pm." "What can I realistically drop today?"

## Example Prompts

Copy and paste these directly into Samantha:

- "What's on my plate today? Give me a prioritised plan."
- "Summarise my meetings for today and flag any conflicts."
- "What are my top 3 tasks I should finish before noon?"
- "Block 90 mins for deep work somewhere in my afternoon."
- "What did I not finish yesterday that I should move to today?"
- "Give me a morning brief -  calendar, tasks, and anything urgent."
- "What's the most important thing I should do first this morning?"
- "Help me plan my day around my 2 pm and 4 pm meetings."
- "What can I realistically get done today, given my meetings?"
- "Wrap up my day - what did I finish and what carries over tomorrow?"

## Setup Requirements

- **Connect:** Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar
- **Optional:** Connect Gmail so Samantha can flag urgent emails in your plan

## Tips to Get Better Results

- Tell Samantha your top 1–3 priorities each morning for a sharper plan
- Set a recurring "morning brief" reminder so it runs automatically
- Define what "urgent" means for you (e.g. client-facing, revenue-related)
- Use the evening wrap-up to feed tomorrow's plan
- Be specific about constraints: "I have no focus time after 3pm"

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Plan my day" (too vague) | Add context: "Plan my day, I have a client call at 2 pm and a proposal due EOD" |
| Not connecting Calendar | Samantha can't see meetings without Calendar access |
| Skipping the evening wrap | Tomorrow's plan is weaker without knowing what carried over |

## FAQs

**Can Samantha automatically block time on my calendar?** Yes, if you ask it to. Say "Block 90 mins for deep work this morning," and it will add it to your calendar.

**Will it reschedule my meetings?** Only if you ask. Samantha suggests changes, but won't move anything without your confirmation.

**Can I get this as a daily digest automatically?** Yes,  set a recurring reminder in MySamantha to trigger your morning brief each day.
