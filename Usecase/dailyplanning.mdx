Turn your scattered tasks, calendar, and emails into a focused daily plan before your first meeting.

## What is Daily Planning?

Daily Planning helps you pull together your meetings, tasks, and follow-ups into one realistic plan so you know what matters most before the day gets busy.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Best For" icon="calendar-days">
    - Busy professionals who start the day unsure of what to tackle first
    - Anyone juggling meetings, tasks, and emails across multiple tools
  </Card>

  <Card title="What You’ll Get" icon="list-checks">
    - a quick view of today’s meetings and deadlines
    - a realistic list of top priorities
    - help spotting conflicts or overloaded time
    - suggested focus blocks around meetings
    - support carrying unfinished work into tomorrow
    - a cleaner end-of-day reset
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How daily planning works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Step 1: Start with a daily brief">
    Ask Samantha for a quick overview of your day.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Give me my plan for today.
    
    What’s on my plate today?
    
    Summarize my meetings and top tasks for today.
    ```

    Samantha can pull together:

    - today’s calendar events
    - pending and overdue tasks
    - important follow-ups
    - open time in your schedule
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 2: Find what matters most">
    Once you see the full picture, Samantha can help you narrow it down.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    What are my top 3 priorities today?
    
    What should I finish before noon?
    
    What can I realistically get done today?
    ```

    This helps turn a long list into a practical plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 3: Protect time for real work">
    If your day is packed, Samantha can help you make space.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Block 90 minutes for deep work this afternoon.
    
    Do I have any free time between meetings?
    
    Where can I fit focused work today?
    ```

    This is useful for protecting time before your day gets fragmented.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 4: Adjust when the day changes">
    Plans shift. Samantha can help you update without rebuilding everything.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    What should I move to tomorrow?
    
    What can I drop today?
    
    Reschedule this task around my meetings.
    ```

    This helps keep the day realistic instead of overloaded.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 5: Close the day cleanly">
    At the end of the day, Samantha can help you review what got done and what carries forward.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Wrap up my day.
    
    What did I finish today?
    
    What should carry over to tomorrow?
    ```

    This makes the next day easier to start.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Share your top 1 to 3 priorities each morning for a sharper daily plan.
</Tip>
