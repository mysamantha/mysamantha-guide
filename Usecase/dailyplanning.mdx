---
title: "Daily Planning"
---

Turn your scattered tasks, calendar, and emails into a focused daily plan before your first meeting.

## Best For

- Busy professionals who start the day unsure of what to tackle first
- Anyone juggling meetings, tasks, and emails across multiple tools

## What You'll Get

- A prioritised list of tasks for the day
- A clear view of your meetings and free time blocks
- Suggestions on what to defer, delegate, or drop
- An evening wrap-up to close out the day cleanly

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Ask for your daily brief**

> "Give me my plan for today. Summarise my meetings, flag any conflicts, and list my top tasks by priority."

**Step 2: Samantha builds your plan**

- Pulls today's calendar events and pending tasks
- Flags scheduling conflicts or back-to-back meetings
- Suggests time blocks for deep work

**Step 3: Adjust as needed**

> "Block 90 mins for deep work this afternoon." "What can I realistically drop today?"

## Example Prompts

1. "What's on my plate today? Give me a prioritised plan."
2. "Summarise my meetings for today and flag any conflicts."
3. "What are my top 3 tasks I should finish before noon?"
4. "Block 90 mins for deep work somewhere in my afternoon."
5. "What did I not finish yesterday that I should move to today?"
6. "Give me a morning brief - calendar, tasks, and anything urgent."
7. "What can I realistically get done today, given my meetings?"
8. "Wrap up my day - what did I finish and what carries over tomorrow?"

## Tips

- Share your top 1–3 priorities each morning for a sharper plan
- Set a recurring morning brief reminder so it runs automatically
- Use the evening wrap-up to feed tomorrow's plan
