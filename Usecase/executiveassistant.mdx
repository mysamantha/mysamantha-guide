Samantha acts like a personal executive assistant by bringing together your calendar, priorities, inbox, and key decisions so you know what matters before the day gets busy.

## **Best for**

- founders, executives, and operators with packed schedules
- people who lose the first part of the day figuring out what matters
- professionals who want one daily command view instead of checking multiple apps
- anyone who wants to stay proactive instead of reactive

## **What you’ll get**

- a morning brief with your calendar, tasks, and urgent emails in one view
- your top priorities surfaced clearly
- flags for anything urgent, overdue, or blocked
- help protecting time for focused work
- an evening wrap so tomorrow starts cleaner

## **How executive assistant mode works**

### **Step 1: Start with a morning brief**

Ask Samantha to give you a clear view of the day.

**For example:**

```text

Give me my morning brief for today.

What are the 3 most important things I need to do today?

Summarize everything I need to know before my first meeting.
```

**Samantha can help pull together:**

- today’s meetings
- top tasks
- urgent emails
- overdue items
- important follow-ups
- open decisions or blockers

This helps you start with clarity instead of switching between tools.

### **Step 2: Focus on what matters most**

Once you see the full picture, Samantha can help narrow it down.

**For example:**

```text

What should I focus on first today?

What are my top 3 priorities?

What can I defer or delegate today?
```

This helps turn a busy day into a more realistic plan.

### **Step 3: Protect your time**

If your day is crowded, Samantha can help you make space for actual work.

**For example:**

```text

Block 2 hours for deep work before my 11 AM call.

Do I have prep time before my afternoon meetings?

Where can I fit focused work today?
```

This helps prevent the day from being fully consumed by meetings and interruptions.

### **Step 4: Surface decisions and blockers**

Samantha can also help you see what needs your input before it becomes a bottleneck.

**For example:**

```text

What decisions are waiting for me right now?

What is blocked that needs my attention?

What urgent emails or approvals should I handle this morning?
```

This is useful when your role depends on fast decisions and follow-through.

### **Step 5: Close the day cleanly**

At the end of the day, Samantha can help you reset for tomorrow.

**For example:**

```text

Give me my evening wrap.

What did I finish today and what carries over?

What should I focus on tomorrow morning?
```

This makes the next day easier to start with less friction.

### **Example prompts**

```text

Give me my morning brief for today.

What are the 3 most important things I need to do today?

What meetings do I have today, and do I have prep time for each?

Are there any urgent emails I need to handle this morning?

What is overdue that I need to deal with today?

Block focus time before my first meeting.

What can I delegate today to free up my time?

Give me my evening wrap with wins, carry-overs, and tomorrow’s priorities.

What decisions are waiting for me right now?

Summarize everything I need to know before my 10 AM call.
```

### **Tips for better results**

<Tip>
  - Ask for your morning brief at the same time each day
  - Share your top priority the night before, when possible
  - Use focus blocks to protect real work time
  - Ask Samantha to surface blockers early
  - End the day with a wrap so tomorrow starts cleaner
</Tip>
