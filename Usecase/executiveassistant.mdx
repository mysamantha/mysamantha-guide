Samantha acts as your personal executive assistant, pulling together everything you need to know and do before your day begins.

## Best For

- Founders, executives, and operators with packed schedules
- Anyone who wastes the first hour of their day figuring out what to focus on
- Professionals who want a single daily brief instead of checking 5 different apps

## What You'll Get

- A morning brief covering your calendar, tasks, and inbox in one view
- Your top 3 priorities for the day surfaced automatically
- Flags on anything urgent, overdue, or needing a decision
- An evening wrap to close the day and prep for tomorrow

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Trigger your morning brief**

> "Give me my executive brief for today calendar, top tasks, urgent emails, and my focus for the day."

**Step 2: Samantha pulls it all together**

- Summarizes today's meetings and flags conflicts
- Surfaces the 3 most important tasks to finish today
- Highlights any urgent emails that need attention
- Flags decisions or blockers that need your input

**Step 3: Set your intention**

> "Block 2 hours for deep work before my 11am call." "What can I delegate or defer today?" "Give me my evening wrap what did I finish and what's tomorrow looking like?"

<Accordion title="Example Prompts ">
  Give me my morning brief for today

  What are the 3 most important things I need to do today?

  What meetings do I have today, and do I have prep time for each?

  Are there any urgent emails I need to handle this morning?

  What's overdue that I need to deal with today?

  Block focus time before my first meeting.

  What can I delegate today to free up my time?

  Give me my evening wrap wins, carry-overs, and tomorrow's priorities.

  What decisions are waiting for me right now?

  Summarize everything I need to know before my 10 am call.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Set a recurring daily brief every morning at a fixed time

  Tell Samantha your top priority the night before for a sharper morning plan

  Use the evening wrap every day,  it makes the next morning brief much more accurate
</Tip>
