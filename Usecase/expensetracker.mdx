Turn everyday spending into a simple system so Samantha can help you log expenses, organize them, and review where your money goes.

## **Best For**

- Anyone tracking personal or work spending
- People who want a lightweight expense log
- Anyone who wants quick summaries without another app

## **What You’ll Get**

- A running expense log
- Clearer spending categories
- Weekly or monthly totals
- Quick summaries by category
- Better visibility into spending habits

## **How expense tracking works**

### **Step 1: Start your tracker**

Create one place to capture spending.

**For example:**

```text
Create an expense tracker for May with categories for food, travel, bills, and shopping.

Start a weekly expense log for me.

Set up a simple personal expense tracker.
```

This gives you a simple place to keep all your expense entries together.

### **Step 2: Log expenses quickly**

Add expenses as they happen.

**For example:**

```text
Add ₹250 for lunch to my expense tracker.

Log ₹1200 for groceries today.

Add a taxi expense of ₹180.
```

This makes it easy to keep the tracker current without extra effort.

### **Step 3: Organize your entries**

Ask Samantha to group and clean up the tracker.

**For example:**

```text
Categorize my expenses from this week.

Group my spending into food, travel, bills, and shopping.

Organize this tracker by category.
```

This helps turn raw entries into something easier to review.

### **Step 4: Review totals**

Get a quick summary of where your money is going.

**For example:**

```text
How much did I spend this week?

Summarize my spending by category.

What are my biggest expenses this month?
```

This helps you understand the bigger picture instead of just individual entries.

### **Step 5: Spot patterns**

Use Samantha to notice trends and overspending.

**For example:**

```text
Where am I spending the most?

Did I overspend on food this month?

Show me any unusual expenses.
```

This makes the tracker useful for decisions, not just record-keeping.

<Tip>
  Track expenses as you spend, not later.\
  Use one tracker per week or month for cleaner summaries.\
  Review category totals regularly to spot patterns early.
</Tip>
