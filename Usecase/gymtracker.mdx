## What is Gym Tracker?

Gym Tracker helps you keep workouts, exercises, and training progress organized in one place so you can stay consistent and review how you are doing over time.

## How gym tracking works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Step 1: Start your tracker">
    Create one place to log workouts and routines.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Create a gym tracker for this week.
    
    Start a workout log for me.
    
    Set up a gym tracker with days, exercises, sets, and reps.
    ```

    This gives you a clear place to record each workout session.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 2: Log your workouts">
    Add exercises and workout details as you go.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Log today’s workout: chest and triceps.
    
    Add bench press 3 sets of 10 to my gym tracker.
    
    Record my leg day workout for today.
    ```

    This makes it easy to keep your training log up to date.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 3: Keep your workout log structured">
    Ask Samantha to organize your entries clearly.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Organize this week’s workouts by day.
    
    Format my gym tracker with exercises, sets, and reps.
    
    Clean up my workout log for this week.
    ```

    This makes your tracker easier to review later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 4: Review your progress">
    Check what you’ve completed so far.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Summarize my workouts from this week.
    
    How many times did I go to the gym this week?
    
    What muscle groups did I train this week?
    ```

    This helps you stay aware of your effort and consistency.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 5: Spot patterns and stay consistent">
    Use Samantha to review habits over time.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Am I staying consistent with my workouts?
    
    Which workout days did I miss this week?
    
    Show me my gym pattern over the last two weeks.
    ```

    This helps turn a workout log into a useful fitness habit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Use one tracker per week for easier review and a clearer view of workout consistency.
</Tip>

## Tips for gym tracking

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Log workouts right after training" icon="clock">
    Log workouts right after each session for better accuracy.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Keep your structure consistent" icon="list">
    Use the same format for days, exercises, sets, and reps so your log stays easy to review.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Review progress weekly" icon="chart-line">
    Weekly reviews make it easier to notice consistency, missed sessions, and training patterns.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Use Samantha for summaries" icon="sparkles">
    Ask Samantha to summarize workout history instead of scanning old entries manually.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
