Samantha helps you coordinate interviews, draft follow-ups, track where candidates stand, and keep the hiring process moving without losing momentum.

## **Best for**

- Founders and managers hiring without a dedicated recruiter
- Teams coordinating multiple candidates at once
- People who want faster scheduling and cleaner follow-up
- Anyone trying to avoid losing candidates to slow responses

## **What you’ll get**

- interviews scheduled with less back-and-forth
- follow-up emails drafted faster
- a clearer view of where each candidate stands
- reminders so no candidate goes too long without a response
- interview notes turned into usable next steps
- a more organized hiring process overall

## **How hiring and recruiting works**

### **Step 1: Start with the hiring context**

Give Samantha the context for the role or candidate pipeline.

**For example:**

```text

I’m interviewing 4 candidates for a product manager role this week.

Help me stay on top of hiring for this role.

Show me what I need to do next for these candidates.
```

Samantha can help organize:

- upcoming interviews
- candidate follow-ups
- interview notes
- open hiring tasks
- where each candidate is in the process

### **Step 2: Schedule interviews faster**

Samantha can help reduce the back-and-forth around interview coordination.

**For example:**

```text

Schedule interviews with 3 candidates this week, 45 minutes each.

What interviews do I have this week, and do I have prep time before each?

Find time for final-round interviews next week.
```

This helps keep the process moving instead of getting delayed by calendar friction.

### **Step 3: Keep candidate communication moving**

Once interviews are scheduled or completed, Samantha can help with outreach and follow-ups.

**For example:**

```text

Draft a friendly follow-up email after a first-round interview.

Draft a rejection email, keep it warm and encouraging.

Draft an offer email for the candidate we’re moving forward with.
```

This makes it easier to respond quickly and consistently.

### **Step 4: Capture interview notes and decisions**

After interviews, Samantha can help you turn rough notes into something more useful.

**For example:**

```text

Summarize my notes from today’s interviews and highlight key points.

Clean up these interview notes.

Pull out the main strengths, concerns, and next steps.
```

This helps you keep better records and make decisions with more clarity.

### **Step 5: Track next steps so nothing gets stuck**

Hiring slows down when follow-up slips. Samantha can help make sure that does not happen.

**For example:**

```text

Who haven’t I followed up with after their interview?

Create a reminder to follow up with every candidate within 24 hours.

What candidates have been waiting more than 3 days for a reply?
```

This helps prevent strong candidates from going cold.

### **Example prompts**

```text

Schedule interviews with 3 candidates this week, 45 minutes each.

Draft a friendly follow-up email to send after a first-round interview.

Who in my pipeline hasn’t heard back in more than 3 days?

Draft an offer email for the candidate we’re moving forward with.

Create a reminder to follow up with every candidate within 24 hours of their interview.

Summarize my notes from today’s interviews and highlight key points.

Draft a polite rejection email, keep it warm and encouraging.

What interviews do I have this week, and do I have prep time before each?
```

### **Tips for better results**

<Tip>
  Add quick notes after each interview, even if they are rough

  Follow up with candidates consistently and quickly

  Review pipeline status often so no one gets stuck

  Block prep time before interviews when possible

  Use reminders so candidate communication does not slip
</Tip>
