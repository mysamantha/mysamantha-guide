Hiring is time-consuming. Samantha handles the scheduling, follow-ups, and coordination so you can focus on finding the right person.

## Best For

- Founders and managers running hiring without a dedicated recruiter
- Anyone coordinating multiple candidates at the same time
- Teams that lose candidates to slow follow-ups and scheduling delays

## What You'll Get

- Interview scheduling without the back-and-forth
- Follow-up emails drafted and sent on time
- A clear view of where each candidate stands in the pipeline
- Reminders to follow up so no one falls through the cracks

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Share the hiring context**

> "I'm interviewing 4 candidates for a product manager role this week. Help me stay on top of it."

**Step 2: Samantha coordinates**

- Schedules interviews based on your availability
- Drafts outreach and follow-up emails for each candidate
- Tracks where each candidate is in the process

**Step 3: Stay on top of next steps**

> "Who haven't I followed up with after their interview?" "Draft a rejection email for the candidate I spoke to on Tuesday."

<Accordion title="Example Prompts ">
  Schedule interviews with 3 candidates this week, 45 mins each

  Draft a friendly follow-up email to send after a first-round interview

  Who in my pipeline hasn't heard back in more than 3 days?

  Draft an offer email for the candidate we're moving forward with

  Create a reminder to follow up with every candidate within 24 hours of their interview

  Summarize my notes from today's interviews and highlight key points

  Draft a polite rejection email, keep it warm and encouraging

  What interviews do I have this week, and do I have prep time before each?
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Give Samantha a quick note after each interview,  even 3 bullets help

  Set a rule: follow up with every candidate within 24 hours

  Ask Samantha to track pipeline status so nothing gets stuck
</Tip>
