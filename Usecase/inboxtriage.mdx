---
title: "Inbox Triage / Inbox Zero"
---

Stop drowning in email. Let Samantha scan, sort, and draft so you can process your inbox in a fraction of the time.

## Best For

- Busy professionals with 30\+ emails a day
- Mondays, post-travel, or after being out of the office
- Anyone who feels like they're missing something important

## **What you’ll get**

- a clearer view of which emails matter right now
- grouped emails by urgency or action needed
- short summaries of important threads
- draft replies for priority emails
- follow-up tasks for emails waiting on someone else
- a faster path to a cleaner inbox

## **How inbox triage works**

### **Step 1: Start with an inbox scan**

Ask Samantha to review recent emails and surface what needs attention.

**For example:**

```text

Triage my inbox and show me what needs a reply.

Scan my inbox from the last 7 days and group by urgency.

Show only emails that need action today.
```

Samantha can help identify:

- urgent emails
- important but non-urgent emails
- low-priority messages
- emails that can wait
- emails that do not need a reply

### **Step 2: Focus on the important threads**

Once your inbox is organized, Samantha can summarize the most important threads so you do not have to open everything.

**For example:**

```text

Summarize the last 20 emails as bullets.

Show me anything from clients or payments first.

What are the top threads I should deal with now?
```

This helps you understand what matters before you start replying.

### **Step 3: Draft replies faster**

When you know which emails need a response, Samantha can help draft them.

**For example:**

```text

Draft quick replies for these threads in a friendly, direct tone.

Write a polite follow-up for anyone who hasn’t replied since last week.

Draft short replies for the top 3 emails
```

This makes it easier to move through the inbox without writing every message from scratch.

### **Step 4: Turn email into action**

Not every email should stay in your inbox as a reminder.

Samantha can help you turn email into next steps like:

- follow-up tasks
- reminders
- calendar actions
- scheduled replies

**For example:**

```text

Create follow-up tasks for emails older than 5 days with no response.

Remind me to follow up on this next week.

Schedule a meeting based on this thread.
```

This helps keep your inbox from becoming your task list.

### **Step 5: Clear the rest**

Once important threads are handled, you can use Samantha to help clean up what remains.

**For example:**

```text

Label newsletters and move them out of my main inbox.

Archive anything that does not need action.

Help me reach inbox zero without missing anything important.
```

This turns inbox cleanup into a faster decision process instead of a slow manual review.

**Example prompts**

```text

Triage my inbox and show only emails that need a reply.

Scan my inbox from the last 7 days and group by urgency.

Find anything from clients or payments and put them at the top.

Summarize the last 20 emails and suggest the next action.

Draft quick replies for these threads in a friendly, direct tone.

Write a polite chase-up for anyone who hasn’t replied since last week.

Create follow-up tasks for emails waiting on someone else.

Label newsletters and help me clear the rest.

Help me reach inbox zero without missing anything important.
```

## **Tips for better results**

<Tip>
  - Tell Samantha which senders matter most to you
  - Ask for reply-needed emails first before looking at everything else
  - Set a preferred reply style, like short, direct, or formal
  - Turn waiting threads into tasks instead of leaving them in the inbox
  - Separate newsletters and low-value mail from real work emails
</Tip>
