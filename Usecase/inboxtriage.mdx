Stop drowning in email. Let Samantha scan, sort, and draft so you can process your inbox in a fraction of the time.

## Best For

- Founders, operators, EAs, and anyone with 30+ emails a day
- Mondays, post-travel, or after being out of office
- "I'm missing something important" situations

## What You'll Get

- A prioritised inbox: Urgent, Important, Can Wait
- Suggested replies and ready-to-send drafts
- Follow-up tasks for emails waiting on others
- Simple labels to stay organised going forward

## Inputs Samantha Needs

- What "urgent" means for you (clients, finance, deadlines, your boss)
- Any VIP senders or domains to always prioritise
- Your preferred reply tone (short, friendly, formal)
- Whether Samantha can label/archive, or should only suggest

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Ask for a scan**
> "Scan my inbox from the last 7 days. Group by urgency. Flag anything that needs a reply today."

**Step 2: Samantha triages**
- Surfaces top threads and summarises each in 1–2 lines
- Suggests next action: reply, delegate, schedule, or archive
- Drafts replies for the highest priority items

**Step 3: You confirm actions**
> "Send the top 3 replies."
> "Archive everything in 'Can Wait' except invoices."
> "Create follow-up reminders for anything waiting on someone else."

## Example Prompts

- "Triage my inbox. Show only emails that need a reply."
- "Find anything from clients or payments and put them at the top."
- "Draft quick replies for these threads in a friendly, direct tone."
- "Make follow-up tasks for emails older than 5 days with no response."
- "Summarise the last 20 emails as bullets with the next action."
- "Search my inbox for 'invoice', 'payment', 'urgent' and prioritise."
- "Write a polite chase-up for anyone who hasn't replied since last week."
- "Label newsletters and move them out of my main inbox."
- "Help me reach Inbox Zero without missing anything important."
- "What emails need a reply today before 5pm?"

## Setup Requirements

- **Connect:** Gmail or Outlook
- **Optional:** Create labels (Urgent, Waiting On, Newsletters)
- **Decide:** Samantha drafts only vs. drafts + labels/archives

---

## Tips to Get Better Results

- Give 5–10 VIP senders (boss, key clients, finance team)
- Define deadlines: "reply same day" vs "reply this week"
- Use a consistent tone rule: "keep all replies under 4 lines"
- Tell Samantha what not to touch (legal, HR, banking threads)
- Run inbox triage first thing Monday and after any time away

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Clean my inbox" (too broad) | Say "last 7 days, reply-needed only" |
| No priority rules set | Share VIP list + keywords like "invoice", "urgent", "client" |
| Newsletters mixed with work | Label and filter them early, ask Samantha to handle in bulk |

## FAQs

**Can Samantha send emails automatically?**
Only if you explicitly confirm what to send. It will never send without your approval.

**Will it delete anything?**
Not unless you specifically ask it to archive or delete a set of emails.

**Can it handle reply threads, not just individual emails?**
Yes — Samantha reads the full thread context before suggesting a reply.
