---
title: 'Meeting Scheduling + Notes"'
---

Stop losing time to scheduling ping-pong and meetings with no follow-through. Samantha handles the coordination and keeps everyone aligned.

## Best For

- Managers and team leads running multiple meetings a week
- Anyone who loses track of action items after calls
- Teams that need agendas prepped and notes shared fast

## What You'll Get

- Meetings booked without the back-and-forth
- Agendas drafted and sent before each call
- Notes and action items captured after the meeting
- Follow-up tasks with owners and deadlines set automatically

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Ask Samantha to schedule**

> "Schedule a 30-min call with Priya and James this week. Find a time that works for all of us."

**Step 2: Samantha handles it**

- Check your calendar for free slots
- Suggests 2–3 options and sends an invite
- Draft an agenda based on the meeting purpose

**Step 3: Post-meeting - capture notes**

> "Here are my notes from the call. Extract action items and create tasks with owners and deadlines."

<Accordion title="Example Prompts ">
  Schedule a 1:1 with Rohan for Thursday, 30 mins

  Find a time this week for a team sync, avoid Monday mornings

  Draft an agenda for tomorrow's client call. Context: reviewing Q1 results

  These are my meeting notes. Pull out all action items

  Draft a post-meeting summary email to send to all attendees

  What meetings do I have tomorrow, and do I have prep time for each

  Set a reminder to follow up on action items from this meeting in 3 days

  Reschedule my 3 pm to later this week. I need time to prep
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Always give context when asking for agendas ("reviewing a proposal" vs just "client call")

  Paste raw notes immediately after the meeting while they're fresh

  Ask Samantha to assign owners, not just list tasks
</Tip>
