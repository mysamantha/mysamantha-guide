Coordinate meetings, prepare faster, and turn conversations into clear next steps with Samantha.

Instead of juggling scheduling, agendas, notes, and follow-ups across different tools, you can manage the full meeting workflow in one place.

## What is Meeting Scheduling \+ Notes?

Meeting Scheduling \+ Notes helps you coordinate meetings, prepare before the call, capture notes after it ends, and turn conversations into action.

## How it works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Schedule the meeting">
    Ask Samantha to find time and create the meeting.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Schedule a 30-minute call with Priya and James this week.
    
    Find time for a team sync this week, but avoid Monday morning.
    
    Reschedule my 3 PM to later this week.
    ```

    **Samantha can help with:**

    - checking availability
    - avoiding conflicts
    - finding open slots
    - creating the calendar event
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Prepare before the meeting">
    Before the call, Samantha can help you get ready.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Draft an agenda for tomorrow’s client call. We’re reviewing Q1 results.
    
    What meetings do I have tomorrow, and do I have prep time for each?
    
    Block prep time before my afternoon review meeting.
    ```

    This helps you go into meetings with more context and less last-minute scrambling.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Capture notes after the meeting">
    Once the meeting is done, you can turn rough notes into something usable.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Here are my meeting notes. Pull out the key decisions.
    
    Summarize this call in a few bullets.
    
    Clean up these raw notes.
    ```

    This makes meeting notes easier to review and share later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Turn notes into action">
    Samantha can extract next steps from your notes and help you keep momentum.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Extract action items and create tasks with owners and deadlines.
    
    Set a reminder to follow up on this in 3 days.
    
    Turn these notes into a task list.
    ```

    This helps make sure meetings lead to actual progress.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send follow-up">
    If needed, Samantha can also help draft the recap.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Draft a post-meeting summary email.
    
    Write a follow-up with next steps and owners.
    
    Send a recap of this call.
    ```

    This keeps everyone aligned after the meeting ends.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Include the meeting purpose when asking for an agenda so Samantha can prepare something more useful.
</Tip>

## Example prompts

```text
Schedule a 1:1 with Rohan for Thursday, 30 minutes.

Find time this week for a team sync, avoid Monday mornings.

Draft an agenda for tomorrow’s client call. Context: reviewing Q1 results.

What meetings do I have tomorrow, and do I have prep time for each?

These are my meeting notes. Pull out all action items.

Summarize this meeting and draft a follow-up email.

Set a reminder to follow up on action items from this meeting in 3 days.

Reschedule my afternoon meeting so I have prep time first.
```
