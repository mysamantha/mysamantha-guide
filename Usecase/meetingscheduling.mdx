description: "Book meetings, set agendas, and capture action items — without the back-and-forth."

Stop losing time to scheduling ping-pong and meetings with no follow-through. Samantha handles the coordination and keeps everyone aligned.

## Best For

- Managers, team leads, and operators running multiple meetings a week
- Anyone who loses track of action items after calls
- Teams that need agendas prepped and notes shared fast

## What You'll Get

- Meetings booked without the back-and-forth
- Agendas drafted and sent before each call
- Notes and action items captured during or after the meeting
- Follow-up tasks assigned and reminders set automatically

## Inputs Samantha Needs

- Access to your Calendar
- Names and emails of attendees (or a description: "my team", "the client")
- Meeting purpose and any context (optional but improves agenda quality)
- Your preferred meeting length and availability windows

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Ask Samantha to schedule**
> "Schedule a 30-min call with Priya and James this week. Find a time that works for all of us."

**Step 2: Samantha handles it**
- Checks your calendar for free slots
- Suggests 2–3 options and sends an invite
- Drafts an agenda based on the meeting purpose

**Step 3: Post-meeting — capture notes**
> "Here are my notes from the call. Extract action items and create tasks with owners and deadlines."

## Example Prompts

- "Schedule a 1:1 with Rohan for Thursday, 30 mins."
- "Find a time this week for a team sync — avoid Monday mornings."
- "Draft an agenda for tomorrow's client call. Context: we're reviewing Q1 results."
- "Send a calendar invite to everyone with the agenda attached."
- "These are my meeting notes — pull out all action items."
- "Who owns what after today's standup? Summarise."
- "Set a reminder to follow up on action items from this meeting in 3 days."
- "Reschedule my 3pm to later this week — I need time to prep."
- "Draft a post-meeting summary email to send to all attendees."
- "What meetings do I have tomorrow and do I have prep time for each?"

## Setup Requirements

- **Connect:** Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar
- **Connect:** Gmail or Outlook (for sending invites and follow-up emails)
- **Optional:** Connect your notes app for automatic note syncing

---

## Tips to Get Better Results

- Always give context when asking for agendas ("we're reviewing a proposal" vs. just "client call")
- Paste raw notes into Samantha immediately after the meeting while they're fresh
- Ask Samantha to assign owners, not just list tasks
- Use "follow up in X days" reminders for accountability
- For recurring meetings, set a template agenda Samantha can reuse

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Schedule a meeting" (no context) | Add who, how long, and when: "30 mins with Priya, this Thursday afternoon" |
| Not reviewing the agenda before sending | Ask Samantha to draft first, then confirm before it sends |
| Skipping post-meeting action capture | Paste notes right after — even messy bullet points work |

---

## FAQs

**Can Samantha send calendar invites directly?**
Yes, if you've connected your Calendar and confirm the details before sending.

**Will it join the meeting and take notes automatically?**
Not yet — you paste notes or a transcript and Samantha extracts the structure.

**Can it send the follow-up email to all attendees?**
Yes — ask it to draft and confirm before sending.
