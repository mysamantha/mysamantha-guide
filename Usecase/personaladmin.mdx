Stay on top of bills, appointments, renewals, and everyday life logistics without letting them pile up.

Samantha helps you keep personal admin visible, organized, and timed properly so small tasks do not turn into urgent problems.

## What is Personal Admin?

Personal Admin helps you manage bills, appointments, renewals, errands, and follow-ups in one place so life logistics stay under control.

## How personal admin works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Step 1: Add what’s coming up">
    Start by telling Samantha about upcoming personal tasks, deadlines, or appointments.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Remind me to pay my credit card bill on the 25th every month.
    
    My car insurance renews in 3 weeks. Remind me to review it.
    
    Add a reminder to pick up my passport before the 15th.
    ```

    This helps you get important life admin out of your head and into a system.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 2: Set recurring reminders once">
    For anything that repeats, Samantha can help you set it up once so you do not have to remember it again.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Remind me to pay rent on the 1st of every month.
    
    Set a yearly reminder for my car registration renewal in June.
    
    Remind me to review my gym membership next week.
    ```

    This is useful for bills, subscriptions, renewals, and recurring errands.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 3: Stay ahead of appointments and follow-ups">
    Samantha can also help with personal appointments and the small tasks around them.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Draft a message to reschedule my doctor’s appointment for next Friday.
    
    Remind me to follow up with the landlord about the repair on Thursday.
    
    What appointments do I have coming up this week?
    ```

    This helps you handle personal admin quickly before it becomes stressful.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 4: Review what’s coming up">
    You can ask Samantha for a quick view of your upcoming personal tasks and deadlines.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    What personal tasks do I have this week?
    
    What bills or deadlines do I have in the next 7 days?
    
    What personal admin do I need to deal with soon?
    ```

    This makes it easier to stay ahead instead of reacting at the last minute.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 5: Keep a running personal admin system">
    You do not need to organize everything perfectly upfront. You can keep adding life admin as it comes up and review it regularly.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Add this to my personal tasks.
    
    What should I take care of this weekend?
    
    Give me a personal admin check-in for this week.
    ```

    This helps turn scattered reminders into a simple routine.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Set recurring reminders once for anything that repeats so you do not have to keep recreating the same life admin tasks.
</Tip>
