Personal admin is easy to ignore until it becomes urgent. Samantha keeps track of the small stuff so it never catches you off guard.

## Best For

- Busy professionals who let personal tasks pile up
- Anyone juggling appointments, bills, renewals, and errands alongside work
- People who want one place to track life logistics

## What You'll Get

- Reminders for bills, renewals, and appointments before they're due
- A running list of personal tasks separate from work
- Quick drafts for personal emails and follow-ups
- Alerts for anything time-sensitive coming up

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Tell Samantha what's coming up**

> "Remind me to pay my credit card bill on the 25th every month." "My car insurance renews in 3 weeks - remind me to review it."

**Step 2: Samantha tracks and reminds**

- Sets recurring reminders for regular tasks
- Flags upcoming deadlines before they're due
- Surfaces personal tasks alongside your workday

**Step 3: Handle it fast**

> "What personal tasks do I have this week?" "Draft an email to reschedule my dentist appointment."

<Accordion title="Example Prompts ">
  Remind me to pay rent on the 1st of every month

  My gym membership renews next week - add a reminder to review it

  What personal tasks do I have coming up this week?

  Draft a message to reschedule my doctor's appointment for next Friday.

  Add a reminder: pick up passport before the 15th.

  Remind me to follow up with the landlord about the repair on Thursday.

  Set a yearly reminder for my car registration renewal in June.

  What bills or deadlines do I have in the next 7 days?
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Set up recurring reminders once and let Samantha handle them automatically

  Keep a running "personal inbox", dump anything life-related, and sort later

  Ask for a weekly personal admin check-in so nothing slips
</Tip>
