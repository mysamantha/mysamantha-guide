---
title: "Task Capture + Reminders"
---

Tasks get lost in  email threads, and meeting notes. Samantha pulls them all together so nothing falls through the cracks.

## Best For

- Busy professionals managing tasks across email, chat, and meetings
- Anyone who says "I'll remember that" and then doesn't
- People who want reminders that show up at the right moment

## What You'll Get

- Tasks captured from emails, notes, and conversations in one shot
- Every task organised with a clear owner, priority, and deadline
- Reminders triggered at the right time not just when you ask
- A single up-to-date task list across all your work

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Dump it to Samantha**

> "Add these tasks from my meeting notes: \[paste notes\]" "Remind me to send the proposal to Acme by Thursday 4pm."

**Step 2: Samantha captures and organises**

- Extracts individual tasks from messy text
- Sets deadlines, assigns priorities, flags blockers

**Step 3: Review and adjust**

> "Show me everything due today." "What's overdue?" "Move the Acme deadline to Friday."

## Example Prompts

- "Add a task: follow up with the design team about the homepage."
- "Extract all action items from these meeting notes: \[paste\]"
- "Remind me to submit my expense report every Friday at 4pm."
- "What tasks are due this week? Sort by priority."
- "Move everything due today that I haven't started to tomorrow morning."
- "Which tasks have no deadline? Help me set one for each."
- "Create a recurring task: standup prep every Monday at 8:45am."
- "Flag any tasks I've had open for more than 7 days."

## Tips

- Dump messy notes and let Samantha extract - don't filter yourself
- Always include a rough deadline, even "this week" helps prioritise
- Ask for an overdue review every Monday to stay on top of slippage
