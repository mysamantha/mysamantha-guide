Tasks get lost in Slack messages, email threads, and meeting notes. Samantha pulls them all together and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

## Best For

- Busy professionals managing tasks across email, chat, and meetings
- Anyone who says "I'll remember that" and then doesn't
- People who want reminders that actually show up at the right moment

## What You'll Get

- Tasks captured from emails, notes, and conversations in one shot
- Every task organised with a clear owner, priority, and deadline
- Reminders triggered at the right time — not just when you ask
- A single up-to-date task list across all your work

## Inputs Samantha Needs

- The source of the task (email, meeting notes, voice, or just typing it out)
- A deadline or timeframe (even rough: "this week", "before the client call")
- Priority level (or let Samantha infer it from context)
- Who else is involved (if it's a delegation or follow-up)

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Dump it to Samantha**
> "Add these tasks from my meeting notes: [paste notes]"
> or
> "Remind me to send the proposal to Acme by Thursday 4pm."

**Step 2: Samantha captures and organises**
- Extracts individual tasks from messy text
- Sets deadlines, assigns priorities, flags blockers
- Groups by project or area if relevant

**Step 3: You review and activate**
> "Show me everything due today."
> "Move the Acme proposal deadline to Friday."
> "What's overdue?"

## Example Prompts

- "Add a task: follow up with the design team about the homepage."
- "Extract all action items from these meeting notes: [paste]"
- "Remind me to submit my expense report every Friday at 4pm."
- "What tasks are due this week? Sort by priority."
- "I just got off a call — here's what I need to do: [bullet list]. Add them all."
- "Move everything due today that I haven't started to tomorrow morning."
- "Which tasks have no deadline? Help me set one for each."
- "Create a recurring task: team standup prep every Monday at 8:45am."
- "Flag any tasks I've had open for more than 7 days."
- "What did I commit to in my last 3 meetings that I haven't done yet?"

## Tips to Get Better Results

- Don't filter yourself — dump messy notes and let Samantha extract tasks
- Always include a rough deadline, even "this week" helps prioritise
- Use recurring tasks for anything you do on a schedule
- Ask for an overdue review every Monday to stay on top of slippage
- Name the person responsible clearly: "Follow up with James" not just "follow up"

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Adding vague tasks ("do the thing") | Be specific: "Send Q1 report to Sarah by Thursday" |
| No deadlines set | Even rough timeframes help: "this week", "before Friday standup" |
| Not reviewing the task list regularly | Ask Samantha for a daily or weekly task digest |

---

## FAQs

**Can Samantha create tasks from my emails automatically?**
Yes — ask it to scan recent emails and extract anything that looks like a commitment or to-do.

**Can I set recurring tasks?**
Yes. Say "remind me every Monday at 9am to prep for standup" and it will repeat.

**Will Samantha remind me even if I don't ask?**
Yes, if you've set a deadline or a reminder time — Samantha will surface it at the right moment.
