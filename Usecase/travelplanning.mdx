Plan work trips without the usual chaos. Samantha helps you organize travel, meetings, prep, and follow-ups in one place.

Instead of managing flights, meetings, reminders, and trip notes separately, you can use Samantha to keep the whole trip connected before, during, and after travel.

## What is Travel Planning?

Travel Planning helps you organize work trips in one place so meetings, prep, travel time, and follow-ups stay connected.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Best for" icon="plane">

    - anyone who wants less travel stress and fewer missed details
    - teams managing multi-stop or tightly scheduled travel

## How travel planning works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Step 1: Start with the trip details">
    Give Samantha the basic travel context.

    **For example:**

    - “I’m traveling to New York on March 28 for 2 days.”
    - “I have 3 client meetings during this trip.”
    - “Help me plan this work trip.”

    **Samantha can help organize:**

    - travel dates
    - meetings during the trip
    - prep tasks before departure
    - buffer time around travel
    - follow-ups for after the trip
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 2: Build the itinerary around real time">
    Once the trip details are clear, Samantha can help map your schedule more realistically.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Block travel time to and from the airport on my calendar.
    
    Find a 30-minute gap in my travel day for a quick check-in call.
    
    Do I have any tight turnarounds during this trip?
    ```

    This helps prevent overbooking and gives your trip a more realistic flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 3: Prepare before you leave">
    Before the trip, Samantha can help you get ready.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    What do I need to prep before my client visit on Thursday?
    
    Create a pre-travel checklist for my New York trip.
    
    Remind me what I need before I leave tomorrow.
    ```

    This is useful for making sure the trip is prepared, not just scheduled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 4: Stay organized during the trip">
    Once you are travelling, Samantha can help you stay on top of the day.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    I just landed. What meetings do I have today and where?
    
    What does the rest of my day look like?
    
    What is the next thing I need to be ready for?
    ```

    This keeps the trip useful in practice, not just well planned in advance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 5: Wrap up when the trip ends">
    After the trip, Samantha can help you turn the trip into follow-through.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Summarize all follow-ups from my trip this week.
    
    Pull tasks from my trip meetings and create reminders.
    
    Remind me to submit my travel expenses when I get back Friday.
    ```

    This helps you avoid losing momentum once the travel is over.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Add meeting times as soon as travel is confirmed so Samantha can help build a more realistic trip plan around them.
</Tip>
