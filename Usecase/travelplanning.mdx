title: "Travel Planning"
description: "Plan trips and stay organized on the road without the chaos."
---

Business trip coming up? Samantha handles the itinerary, prep, and follow-ups so you can focus on the meetings that matter.

## Best For

- Professionals who travel frequently for work
- Anyone coordinating multi-stop trips or team travel
- People who lose time to travel logistics instead of actual work

## What You'll Get

- A complete trip itinerary with flights, hotels, and meetings
- Pre-travel checklists so nothing gets forgotten
- Calendar blocks for travel time and buffer windows
- Post-trip follow-up tasks are ready for action

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Share your trip details**

> "I'm traveling to New York on March 28 for 2 days. I have 3 client meetings. Help me plan the trip."

**Step 2: Samantha builds the itinerary**

- Maps out meetings against travel time
- Flags scheduling conflicts or tight turnarounds
- Suggests prep tasks before each meeting

**Step 3: Wrap up after the trip**

> "I'm back from New York. Pull all follow-up tasks from my meetings and create reminders."

<Accordion title="Example prompts ">
  "Plan my trip to San Francisco next Tuesday,  I have meetings at 10 am and 3 pm."

  "What do I need to prep before my client visit on Thursday?"

  "Block travel time to and from the airport on my calendar."

  "Create a pre-travel checklist for my New York trip."

  "I just landed. What meetings do I have today and where?"

  "Summarize all follow-ups from my trip this week."

  "Find a 30-min gap in my travel day for a quick check-in call."

  "Remind me to submit my travel expenses when I get back Friday."
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Always ask for a pre-travel checklist 24 hours before departure

  Use the post-trip wrap to capture all follow-ups while they're fresh
</Tip>
