Samantha helps you plan your day, track personal routines, manage client work, and stay organized without juggling multiple tools.

## Browse by use case

### Personal

- [**Expense Tracker**](/Usecase/expensetracker)\
  Track spending, organize expenses, and review where your money goes.
- [**Calorie Tracker**](/Usecase/calorietracker)\
  Log meals, review calorie intake, and stay consistent with nutrition habits.
- [**Gym Tracker**](/Usecase/gymtracker)\
  Track workouts, routines, and weekly progress in one place.
- [**Travel Planning**](/Usecase/travelplanning)\
  Organize trip details, plans, and reminders for smoother travel.
- [**Personal Admin**](/Usecase/personaladmin)\
  Keep errands, follow-ups, and everyday admin organized.

### Planning

- [**Daily Planning**](/Usecase/dailyplanning)\
  Turn tasks, meetings, and priorities into a focused plan for the day.
- [**Task Capture \+ Reminders**](/Usecase/taskcapture)\
  Quickly save tasks and make sure nothing important gets missed.
- [**Weekly Review**](/Usecase/weeklyreview)\
  Review what got done, what slipped, and what to focus on next.
- [**Content Calendar**](/Usecase/contentcalendar)\
  Plan ideas, deadlines, and publishing schedules in one workflow.

### Work

- [**Inbox Triage / Inbox Zero**](/Usecase/inboxtriage)\
  Clear email faster and focus on what actually needs attention.
- [**Meeting Scheduling \+ Notes**](/Usecase/meetingscheduling)\
  Schedule meetings, capture notes, and keep follow-ups connected.
- [**Client CRM**](/Usecase/clientcrm)\
  Manage client details, conversations, and follow-ups in one place.
- [**Client Project Updates**](/Usecase/clientprojectupdates)\
  Track project status, notes, and next steps across client work.
- [**Hiring & Recruiting**](/Usecase/hiringrecruiting)\
  Organize candidates, interview notes, and hiring follow-ups.
