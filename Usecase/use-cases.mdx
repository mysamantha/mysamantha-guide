Samantha helps you plan smarter, clear your inbox, capture tasks, and run meetings — so you can focus on what matters.

## Pick a use case below

[**Daily Planning**](/UseCases/DailyPlanning)
Start every day with a clear, prioritised plan — tasks, meetings, and focus time in minutes.

[**Inbox Triage / Inbox Zero**](/UseCases/InboxTriage)
Turn a messy inbox into a prioritised list with drafts and follow-ups ready to send.

[**Meeting Scheduling + Notes**](/UseCases/MeetingScheduling)
Book meetings, set agendas, and capture action items without the back-and-forth.

[**Task Capture + Reminders**](/UseCases/TaskCapture)
Never lose a to-do again — capture tasks from anywhere and get reminded at the right time.
