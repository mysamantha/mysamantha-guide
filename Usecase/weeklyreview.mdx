Close out your week with clarity. Samantha helps you review what got done, what is still open, and what deserves attention next week.

Instead of ending the week with loose ends and mental clutter, you can use Samantha to reflect, reset, and start the next week with a clearer plan.

## What is Weekly Review?

Weekly Review helps you look back on your tasks, meetings, and follow-ups so you can see what got done, what slipped, and what deserves attention next week.

## How weekly review works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Step 1: Start with a weekly review">
    Ask Samantha for a quick summary of your week.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Give me a review of this week.
    
    What did I complete this week and what is still open?
    
    Summarize my week and tell me what needs attention.
    ```

    **Samantha can help pull together:**

    - completed tasks
    - unfinished or overdue tasks
    - meetings from the week
    - important follow-ups
    - open work that may need to carry over
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 2: See what slipped">
    Once the week is summarized, Samantha can help you spot what did not get finished.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    What tasks did I not complete this week?
    
    What should I carry over to next week?
    
    What has been open for too long?
    ```

    This helps you review unfinished work on purpose instead of rediscovering it later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 3: Notice patterns and blockers">
    A good weekly review is not just about what got done. It also helps you see what keeps getting in the way.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    What blockers came up this week?
    
    Did anything keep getting pushed?
    
    What recurring tasks did I miss this week?
    ```

    This is useful for noticing patterns, not just managing output.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 4: Plan next week more clearly">
    After reviewing the week, Samantha can help you set direction for the next one.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Based on this week, what should my top 3 priorities be next week?
    
    Move all unfinished tasks to next week and reschedule them.
    
    Help me plan next week based on what is left over.
    ```

    This turns a review into a reset, not just a recap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Step 5: Start the next week with less friction">
    Once your priorities are clearer, Samantha can help you prepare Monday before it arrives.

    **For example:**

    ```text
    Set up my Monday around these priorities.
    
    Give me a clean starting plan for next week.
    
    What should I focus on first next week?
    ```

    This makes the next week easier to enter with intention.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Do your weekly review near the end of the week, like Friday afternoon, while the details are still fresh.
</Tip>

## Example prompts

```text
Summarize my week. What did I finish and what is still open?

What tasks did I not complete this week?

What were my top wins this week?

What should I carry over to next week?

Give me a weekly digest of my meetings and key decisions.

What recurring tasks did I miss this week?

Help me plan next week based on what is left over.

What blockers came up this week that I need to address?
```
