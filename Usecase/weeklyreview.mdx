Stop letting weeks blur together. Samantha helps you review what happened, spot what slipped, and walk into Monday with a clear head.

## Best For

- Busy professionals who lose track of progress week to week
- Anyone who wants to stay on top of goals and not just tasks
- Managers who need a quick summary of their team's week

## What You'll Get

- A summary of completed tasks and meetings from the week
- A list of what slipped and needs to be carried over
- Key wins to note and blockers to address
- A head start on next week's priorities

## Recommended Workflow

**Step 1: Ask for your weekly wrap**

> "Give me a review of this week what I completed, what's still open, and what I should prioritize next week."

**Step 2: Samantha pulls it together**

- Summarizes completed tasks and closed threads
- Surfaces anything overdue or unresolved
- Highlights recurring blockers or patterns

**Step 3: Plan ahead**

> "Based on this week, what should my top 3 priorities be for next week?" "Move all unfinished tasks to next week and reschedule them."

## Example Prompts

- "Summarize my week:  what did I finish and what's still open?"
- "What tasks did I not complete this week?"
- "What were my top wins this week?"
- "What should I carry over to next week?"
- "Give me a weekly digest of my meetings and key decisions."
- "What recurring tasks did I miss this week?"
- "Help me plan next week based on what's leftover."
- "What blockers came up this week that I need to address?"

## Tips

- Do your weekly review every Friday afternoon for the best results
- Ask Samantha to flag anything open for 2\+ weeks
- Use the output to set your Monday morning priorities automatically
