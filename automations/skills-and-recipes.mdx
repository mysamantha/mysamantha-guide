---
title: "Skills"
description: "A Skill saves an instruction you use often, so you can run it in Chat without typing it again."
---

## **Find your Skills**

1. Open **Settings > Skills**.
2. Your saved Skills are listed there.

## **Add a Skill**

1. Select **New skill**.
2. Choose a template, such as **Meeting follow-up**, **Weekly review**, or **Summarize**, or start from scratch.
3. Add a name, description, and instruction, then save.

## **Use a Skill in Chat**

1. Open **Chat** and type `/` to browse your Skills.
2. Select a Skill to run it with your current chat context.

## **Edit a Skill**

1. Go to **Settings > Skills** to manage your saved Skills.
2. You can also ask Samantha in Chat to create or edit a Skill.

## **Example: Meeting summary**

1. Create a Skill called **Meeting summary**.
2. Add the instruction: “Summarize this transcript. List decisions, open questions, and follow-ups with owners and due dates.”
3. Open a meeting transcript in Chat and run the Skill to get a clear summary.
