---
title: "YouDos"
description: "A YouDo in Samantha runs your chosen instruction at a set time or when an event happens."
---

## **How a YouDo works**

1. Choose what starts it, such as a scheduled time, a new email, a note, a journal entry, or a To-Do event.
2. Tell Samantha what to do when that happens.
3. Review the details and confirm before the YouDo is created.

## **Choose a trigger**

1. **Time:** Run once or repeat hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
2. **Email:** Match emails by sender, topic, or other details.
3. **Note or Journal:** Run when an entry is created or changed.
4. **To-Do:** Run when an item is created, completed, or overdue.

## **Give it a clear instruction**

1. Describe the action, not the trigger. For example, “Summarize this email and highlight anything that needs my attention.”
2. For an email YouDo, describe which emails should match, such as “invoices from Stripe.”
3. Choose whether you want a notification. A silent YouDo runs without sending one.

## **Example: Morning briefing**

1. Set the trigger to weekdays at 8:00 a.m.
2. Set the instruction: “Give me a morning briefing with today’s calendar, overdue To-Dos, and important unread emails.”
3. Confirm the schedule and instruction. At the chosen time, Samantha prepares the briefing using your connected information.

Another example: When an invoice from Stripe arrives, a YouDo can summarize it and highlight the amount and due date.

## **Manage your YouDos**

1. View your YouDos, edit their details, pause or resume them, and check their run history.
2. Delete a YouDo only when you no longer need it. Deleting it also removes its run history.

Your emails, calendar, notes, Journals, and To-Dos stay in your Personal Workspace as AI models change. You can use Samantha with that context or use your workspace without AI.

<Note>
  Create and manage YouDos in Settings > Samantha > YouDo
</Note>
