---
title: "Capture from the Web"
description: "Find something useful online? Save the section you need or the whole webpage to Notes, then keep working with that content in MySamantha."
---

## **Capture the content**

1. Open a webpage in Chrome.
2. To save one part, highlight the section you want and open the MySamantha extension.
3. To save the whole page, open the extension and choose to capture the full webpage.

## **Add it to Notes**

1. Save the captured content as a new note if you want to keep it on its own.
2. Add it to an existing note if it belongs with research or notes you’ve already collected.

## **Keep working with the note**

1. Find and revisit it in Notes. Edit it, organize it into a folder, or add a label.
2. Chat with Samantha about the saved content to get a summary, ask questions, or pull out key details and next steps.
3. Ask Samantha to turn those next steps into To-Dos or calendar events when useful.

## **Try it**

1. Save a useful section from a how-to page to an existing project note.
2. Capture a full article as a new note, then ask Samantha for its main points and suggested next steps.
