---
title: "Connect Apps"
description: "Connect your apps to bring their information into MySamantha. Once connected, tools like Samantha can use that context to help you find information and take action."
---

## **Find an app**

1. Open **Settings > Connections**.
2. Search the app catalog or browse its categories.
3. Select an app to see its connection options.

## **Connect an account**

1. Select **Connect** and sign in to the account you want to use.
2. If the app has multiple accounts or workspaces, choose the right one.
3. Review the permissions and approve access to finish connecting.

## **Use a connected app**

1. Ask Samantha to find information or take an action in a connected app. What’s available depends on the app and its permissions.
2. For example, connect your email and ask Samantha to find an invoice and add its due date to your To-Do list.
3. Some actions may ask you to review and confirm them.

## **Manage connections**

1. Return to **Settings > Connections** to view your connected apps.
2. Reconnect an app if its access expires or permissions change.
3. Disconnect it when you no longer want Samantha to use it.

## **If you can’t connect**

1. Check that you selected the right account and completed the permission steps.
2. If you cancelled or the sign-in expired, try connecting again.
