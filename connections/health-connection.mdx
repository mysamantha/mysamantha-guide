---
title: "Connect Health Data "
description: "Connect Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android through the Samantha phone app. You can’t connect them on the web or in chat."
---

## **Connect Apple Health on iPhone**

1. Open the Samantha app on your iPhone.
2. Go to **Profile > Integrations > Apple Health**.
3. Follow the prompts and choose which health data to share.

## **Connect Health Connect on Android**

1. Open the Samantha app on your Android phone.
2. Open the integrations list and select **Health Connect**.
3. Follow the prompts and choose which health data to share.

## **Choose who can use your data**

1. On the health screen, turn on **Assistant can use this** to let Samantha answer questions about your health data. When it’s off, Samantha can’t read or discuss your health numbers.
2. Health applets can still display synced data when Assistant access is off.
3. On iPhone, you can also allow writing to Apple Health. When enabled, Samantha can log water, weight, or mindful minutes when you ask. Samantha does not write to Health Connect.

## **See what syncs**

1. Daily data can include steps, walking and running distance, active energy, exercise minutes, sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, and weight.
2. Depending on what your phone provides, it may also include water, mindful minutes, cardio fitness, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, body fat, flights climbed, and time in daylight.
3. Separate records can show workouts and each night’s main sleep, including sleep times and stages.
4. Health tracking does not include live or minute-by-minute heart rate, routes or GPS, location, food, medication, blood pressure, glucose, or cycle data. Cycle tracking is private and managed on your phone.

## **Keep data fresh**

1. Health data syncs in the background. Android usually syncs about hourly. On iPhone, new data syncs as Apple Health changes and when you open the Samantha app.
2. Today’s numbers can be about an hour behind. To sync now, open the Samantha app or tap **Sync** on the health screen.
