---
title: "Email and Calendar with Samantha "
description: "Manage email and calendar in MySamantha. Find and summarize messages, draft replies, schedule events, and keep conversations and next steps together."
---

## **Connect your accounts**

1. Connect Gmail or Outlook to work with your email.
2. Connect Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar to manage events.
3. MySamantha uses your primary account for new emails and calendar changes. Replies use the account that received the original email.

## **Find and understand emails**

1. Search by sender, subject, date, or topic. For example: “Find the latest email from Maya about the launch review.”
2. Ask Samantha to read an email or thread and summarize its key points, decisions, requests, deadlines, and attachment details.
3. Searches can include your connected email accounts.

## **Draft and manage emails**

1. Ask Samantha to draft a new email, reply, or forward a message.
2. Review and confirm the draft before sending. Drafts created in MySamantha stay there and do not appear in your Gmail or Outlook Drafts folder.
3. Add or remove Gmail labels or Outlook categories, and mark messages as read or unread.

<Note>
  Samantha can read email attachments. 
</Note>

## **Check and manage your calendar**

1. Ask what’s coming up or search for an event. Name a specific calendar if you have more than one.
2. Create or edit events with a title, date, time, location, description, and attendees.
3. Create all-day or recurring events and add a Google Meet link. Review and confirm changes before they're applied.

## **Set reminders and check details**

1. Add reminders when creating or editing an event. Google Calendar supports multiple reminders. Outlook keeps the earliest if you request more than one.
2. Samantha checks for conflicts with existing events and timed To-Dos, then shows them for you to review.
3. For travel or events in another city, use the local time at that location. 
4. Deleting an event moves it to Trash. It is not permanently deleted.

## **Use email and calendar together**

1. A client emails, “Can we review the launch numbers Thursday at 2?”
2. Ask Samantha to find the thread and schedule the review.
3. Samantha identifies the meeting details, checks your calendar, flags any conflict, and prepares a reply and event for you to review.

## **Keep your context in MySamantha**

1. Your emails, calendars, notes, meetings, and next steps stay in your Personal Workspace as AI models change.
2. Use Samantha when you want help taking action, or turn off AI and continue using your workspace.
