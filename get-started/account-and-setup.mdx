---

## **Create an account or sign in**

1. Choose Google, Apple, or email.
2. Verify your email if you sign up with email.
3. Use **Forgot password?** or request a magic link if needed.

## **Complete the setup walkthrough**

1. Choose how to address you and Samantha’s writing tone.
2. Connect your email and calendar. Allow the permissions you need.
3. Explore Notes, To-do, and Journal.
4. Connect other apps or import from Notion or Obsidian.
5. Skip steps and return later. Your progress is saved.

## **Use MySamantha on your devices**

1. Use the web app, iPhone or Android app, or browser extension.
2. Find downloads under **Settings → Get apps & extensions**.
3. The extension supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, and Vivaldi.
