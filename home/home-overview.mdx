## **Use Home**

1. Select a quick action, such as **Check my email**, **Today’s calendar**, **Plan my day**, **Summarize my week**, or **Draft an email**, to start a chat.
2. Built-in tiles include **Greeting**, **Today’s focus**, **Recent chats**, **Personality insights**, and **Journal cue**.
3. Choose **Manage Home** to show or hide built-in tiles.

## **Arrange the board**

1. Select **Arrange**.
2. Drag tiles to move them or resize them with your mouse or keyboard.
3. Select **Done** when you’re finished.

## **Check Home notices**

Home can show notices about disconnected apps, paused YouDos, AI balance, imports, streaks, overdue todos, tips, and announcements. Account notices always appear.

To control tips and announcements, go to **Settings > General** and change **Tips on Home** or **Announcements on Home**.
