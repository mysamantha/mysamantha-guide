---
title: "Personality insights"
description: "The Personality Insights card summarizes patterns from your journal entries, chats, and note titles. It refreshes weekly."
---

## **What the card shows**

1. Your personality type and a chart
2. **Your traits**, which list the traits identified
3. **About you**, a written summary

## **View your insights**

1. Open **Home** and select **Personality insights**.
2. Review your type, chart, traits, and summary.
3. If there isn’t enough writing yet, the card shows **Your personality type is on the way**.
4. Select **Share your personality** to share the card.

## **Share your personality insights**

1. Open **Share your progress**.
2. Add an optional caption, up to 140 characters.
3. Choose whether to show your name and the date.
4. Select **Copy image**, **Download**, or **Get link**. Use **Manage your shared links** to manage links.

<Note>
  Your data stays private. The share card shows the summary, not individual entries.
</Note>
