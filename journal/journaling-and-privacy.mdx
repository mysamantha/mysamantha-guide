title: "Journaling and privacy"
description: "Write and browse journal entries, and learn how Samantha uses your journal and what stays private."
---

## **Write and browse entries**

1. Open Journal and choose a date in **Day**, **Calendar**, or **All entries**.
2. Write an entry. You can add photos or record a voice entry.
3. Voice entries can be up to 60 minutes. 

<Note>
  Without transcription funding, they’re saved as voice notes.
</Note>

4. Edit entries in place. Deleting an entry is permanent; Journal has no Trash.
5. Journal shows your streak and the last two weeks. When health data is connected, a day can also show sleep, steps, and workouts.

## **Your privacy**

1. Your Journal stays in your Personal space and never appears in a workspace.
2. Samantha can read, add, change, or delete entries from chat. Samantha asks first before making changes.
3. Journal entries can inform Memory and Personality insights.
