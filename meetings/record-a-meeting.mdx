---
title: "Record a Meeting"
description: "Record meetings with the MySamantha extension, then upload the audio to create a transcript you can use."
---

## **Prepare to record**

1. Install and sign in to the MySamantha browser extension. See **Install and configure** if you need help.
2. Open the meeting in Chrome.
3. Let participants know you’re recording, and follow any consent requirements that apply.

## **Record the meeting**

1. Open the extension and start recording.
2. Follow any Chrome permission prompts needed for the recording.
3. When the meeting ends, stop the recording so it doesn’t keep running.

## **Create a transcription note**

1. Upload the recording to Notes and start the transcription.
2. Save the transcription as a new note or add it to an existing note.
3. The note includes the transcription, speaker labels, and original audio.

## **Work with the transcription**

1. Chat with Samantha to summarize the discussion or find a specific detail.
2. Ask for decisions, open questions, or clear action items.
3. Ask Samantha to organize the information in the new or existing note.

## **Follow through**

1. Ask Samantha to turn action items into To-Dos.
2. Schedule a follow-up event or prepare an email when needed.
3. Review and confirm before Samantha makes changes or sends anything.
