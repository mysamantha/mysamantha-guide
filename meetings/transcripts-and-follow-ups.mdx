---
title: "Transcripts and Follow-Ups "
description: "A transcript records what was said. A clear summary and follow-ups help you act on it. Save the transcript as a note, then use it to capture decisions, questions, and next steps."
---

## **Review the transcript**

1. Open the meeting note in Notes.
2. Use Chat with Samantha to summarize the meeting, list decisions, or find who said something.
3. Check names, dates, and unclear details against the transcript or audio.

## **Find the follow-ups**

1. Ask Samantha to list each next step with its owner and due date, when stated.
2. If the owner or due date is unclear, ask Samantha to flag it instead of guessing.
3. Turn your own next steps into To-Dos, schedule follow-up events, or draft a recap email.

## **Example**

1. In a project meeting, you agree to send the revised copy by Thursday. A designer agrees to check the mockup by Friday.
2. Ask Samantha: “Summarize the decisions and list the follow-ups with owners and due dates.”
3. Samantha gives you a summary and separates the two follow-ups by owner. You can create a To-Do for your own item and draft a recap email for review.

<Note>
  Review and confirm before Samantha creates events or sends emails.
</Note>
