---
title: "Collaboration and sharing "
description: "Use comments to discuss a note, suggestions to propose edits, and sharing controls to let others read or edit it."
---

## Comment on a note

1. Select text and choose **Comment**.
2. Write a comment. Use **Reply** to continue a thread.
3. Use **Edit**, **Delete**, or **Copy link** to manage a comment. Choose **Resolve** when the discussion is finished, or **Reopen** to continue it.
4. In the comments panel, switch between **All comments**, **For you**, and **Resolved**.
5. Turn on **Suggesting** to propose changes for others to accept or reject. You can also mention someone in a comment.

<Note>
  A note supports up to 1,000 comments, 200 replies in a thread, and 500 open suggestions.
</Note>

## Share or publish a note

1. Choose **Share this note**, then **Publish & copy link**.
2. Use **Copy link** to share it or **Preview** to check what visitors will see.
3. Choose **Make private** to turn off the link.
4. Visitors can read and search the note. After signing in, they can add it to their notes or chat with Samantha. Signed-in visitors can comment, and the owner is notified.
5. If a link is unavailable, visitors may see: **It may be private or no longer shared.**

<Note>
  For a note in a workspace, use the workspace **Share** dialog instead.
</Note>

## Edit a note together

1. Open the **Share** popover and add an editor by email. Editors must have a MySamantha account. You can add up to 10.
2. Editors can change the title, body, and attachments. They can’t access version history, AI, export, or Trash.
3. Presence chips and cursors show who is editing, including Samantha.
4. Check the save status: **All changes saved**, **Syncing…**, or an offline message confirming changes are saved on this device.

<Note>
  Edit together is limited to Personal notes.
</Note>
