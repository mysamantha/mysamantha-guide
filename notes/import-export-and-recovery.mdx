title: "Import, export, and recovery"

---

## Import notes

Start an import from **Settings > Imports & backup**, the **Notes** menu, or during setup.

1. **Notion:** Share the pages with MySamantha, then select the pages to import.
2. **Obsidian:** Choose a vault. MySamantha imports Markdown files, keeps folders, and`[[links]]`skips duplicates. Keep the tab open during upload. Imports run one at a time.
3. **Google Drive:** Import Google Docs,`.txt`and `.md` files as new notes. Very large files may import only the beginning.

<Note>
  Imports are limited to 5,000 files or 500 MB.
</Note>

## **Export or print notes**

1. Open a note and choose a download format: `.docx`, PDF, `.odt`, `.txt`, `.rtf`, `.html`, `.epub`, or `.md`.
2. To save as PDF, use the browser’s print dialog.
3. Choose **Copy as** to copy the note as plain text, Markdown, or HTML.
4. Choose **Export notes** to download all notes as Markdown files in one ZIP.

## **Back up notes**

1. **Save to Google Docs** or **Back up to Notion**.
2. Daily Google Drive backups are saved in a **Samantha Notes Backup** folder. 

## **Restore or delete notes**

1. **Save to Google Docs** or **Back up to Notion**.
2. Daily Google Drive backups are saved in a **Samantha Notes Backup** folder. **Restore or delete notes**
3. Deleted notes and folders go to **Trash**. Deleting a folder also moves its contents to Trash.
4. In Trash, restore one item or select several. Restoring a folder also restores its contents.
5. Choose **Empty Trash** to delete everything in Trash permanently.
