title: "Media and voice "
description: "Add pictures, documents, and audio to notes. Use the Library to find existing content, and record voice notes with transcripts."
---

## **Add media to a note**

1. Open a note and select **Attach file**.
2. Choose **Picture**, **Document**, or **Audio**. Each file can be up to 100 MB.
3. Select an image to rotate, align, wrap, add alt text, download, or expand it.
4. Select **Generate image** to create an image or **AI edit** to edit one. Source images must be PNG, JPG, or WEBP under 25 MB.

<Note>
  In a workspace, attached files count toward shared storage.
</Note>

## **Use the Library**

1. Open **Library** from the sidebar, or choose **From Library** when attaching an item in chat.
2. Browse **All**, **Files**, **Notes**, **To-dos**, **Applets**, or **Chats**. Search, filter, and sort the items.
3. Switch between Gallery and List views. Select an item to preview its summary, open its chat, download it, rename it, or delete it.

<Note>
  Deleting a file removes it from every chat.
</Note>

## **Record a voice note**

1. Start recording in a note or from the Notes list.
2. Use **Pause**, **Delete recording**, or **Labels**. Select **Stop & Transcribe** when finished.
3. Record up to 60 seconds on Free or 60 minutes on other plans.
4. Without transcription funding, the recording is saved as audio only and can be transcribed later.
5. On the audio card, rename speakers or transcribe again. Failed uploads are kept and retried for up to 14 days.
