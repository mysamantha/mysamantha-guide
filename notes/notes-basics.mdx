title: "Notes basics "
description: "Notes help you capture, write, organize, and connect information in MySamantha."
---

## **Create and find notes**

1. Start with **New note**, **Voice note**, **Note from image**, or **Note from file**.
2. Browse **All notes**, **Shared with me**, **Atlas**, or **Trash**.
3. Search, filter, and sort notes. Choose Gallery or List view.

## **Write and organize**

1. Use the toolbar or `/` menu to format text and add links, lists, tables, and more.
2. Group notes with folders and labels. Pin important notes, or move and reorder them.

## **Explore other Notes features**

1. **Tables and forms:** Organize information and collect responses.
2. **Media and voice:** Add images, files, audio, voice notes, and transcripts.
3. **Samantha, links, and Atlas:** Get help with notes, link related notes, and explore their connections.
4. **Collaboration and sharing:** Comment, suggest edits, publish, edit together, and view version history.
5. **Import, export, and recovery:** Import, print, export, back up, and restore notes.
