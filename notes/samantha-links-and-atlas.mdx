description: "Use Samantha to work with note content, link related notes, and explore those connections in Atlas."
---

## Use Samantha in a note

1. Open a note and choose **Generate insight**, or select text to work with just that part.
2. Choose **Summary**, **Explain**, **To-do**, **Email**, **Meeting report**, or **Ask Samantha anything…**
3. Use the Insights panel to **Add to note**, **Copy**, **Save as new note**, or **Delete** the result.
4. Choose **Chat with Samantha** to discuss the note. Notes created with Samantha can receive an automatic title.

<Note>
  AI features require AI to be turned on and funded.
</Note>

## Link notes

1. Type `[[` or choose **Link to note**.
2. Select an existing note. If it doesn’t exist, a pending placeholder is created.
3. Hover over a link to preview the note.
4. Open **Linked from** to see backlinks. Unlinked mentions shows notes that mention this note without linking to it.

## Explore Atlas

1. Open Atlas and use **Find a note**.
2. Select a note to view **Links to**, **Linked from**, **Mentioned not linked**, and **Similar** notes.
3. Use the minimap, zoom, or fit controls to navigate. Click a note to focus on it; double-click to open it.
4. Choose **Tune the atlas** to group notes by label, title, keyword, or topic, and adjust display and force settings.

<Note>
  Atlas settings follow you across devices. If your notes aren’t linked yet, Atlas shows **Your notes don't link to each other yet**.
</Note>
