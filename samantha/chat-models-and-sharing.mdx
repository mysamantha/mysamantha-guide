## **What Samantha can do**

1. Work with Notes, To-dos, Journal, email, calendar, files, and connected apps.
2. Draft email replies and suggest calendar or Journal changes for you to review.
3. Create Notes and To-dos directly. Use **Undo** to reverse changes.

## Use chat

1. Press **Enter** to send, **Shift+Enter** for a new line, and the arrow keys to revisit earlier prompts.
2. Press **Esc** to stop a reply. Messages sent while Samantha is replying are queued.
3. Drafts are saved per chat on your device.
4. Choose **Discuss in chat** from a Note to include it in the conversation.

## Choose a model

1. **Samantha** is the default and selects a model for each question.
2. Choose another model for a message in the model picker.
3. ChatGPT models use your own ChatGPT plan. Models requiring an API key show **Connect** or **Add key**.

## Share a chat

1. Create up to five share links per chat.
2. Recipients can read the chat. Signed-in recipients can continue in their own copy.
3. Choose whether the link stays up to date and includes attachments. Delete a link to stop sharing it.

## Use MySamantha without AI

1. Go to **Settings → General → Turn off AI**.
2. Notes, To-do, and Journal keep working. Samantha, YouDos, and other AI features pause.

<Note>
  **AI is optional.** Turn it off anytime. Notes, To-do, and Journal still work.
</Note>
