## **What Samantha can do**

- Work with Notes, To-dos, Journal, email, calendar, files, and connected apps.
- Draft email replies and suggest calendar or Journal changes for you to review.
- Create Notes and To-dos directly. Use **Undo** to reverse changes.

## Use chat

- Press **Enter** to send, **Shift+Enter** for a new line, and the arrow keys to revisit earlier prompts.
- Press **Esc** to stop a reply. Messages sent while Samantha is replying are queued.
- Drafts are saved per chat on your device.
- Choose **Discuss in chat** from a Note to include it in the conversation.

## Choose a model

- **Samantha** is the default and selects a model for each question.
- Choose another model for a message in the model picker.
- ChatGPT models use your own ChatGPT plan. Models requiring an API key show **Connect** or **Add key**.

## Share a chat

- Create up to five share links per chat.
- Recipients can read the chat. Signed-in recipients can continue in their own copy.
- Choose whether the link stays up to date and includes attachments. Delete a link to stop sharing it.

## Use MySamantha without AI

- Go to **Settings → General → Turn off AI**.
- Notes, To-do, and Journal keep working. Samantha, YouDos, and other AI features pause.

<Info>
  **AI is optional.** Turn it off anytime. Notes, To-do, and Journal still work.
</Info>
