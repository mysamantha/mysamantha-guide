## Memory

1. Samantha builds memory from your Notes, Journal, chats, lists, and emails from people you know.
2. Teach preferences in chat: “Call me…” or “From now on…”
3. Ask what Samantha remembers, or ask her to list or forget a preference.
4. Answer memory questions in Notifications with **Same person**, **Different people**, or **Not sure**. You’ll get at most one a day.

<Info>
  Memory is personal. Each workspace member has their own. Deleting a source removes its related memory.
</Info>

## Proactive messages

1. Samantha may send meeting prep, overdue follow-ups, and important email alerts.
2. Urgent messages arrive right away. Others appear in an end-of-day digest.
3. Find them as unread chats or notifications from **Samantha**.
4. Set **Reply alerts** under **Settings → Email** to hear when someone replies to, or bounces, an email Samantha sent.

<Info>
  Proactive messages pause when AI is off.
</Info>
