---
title: "Recurring To-dos and reminders"
description: "Create repeating To-dos, manage one occurrence or the full series, and set reminders for the right time."
---

## **Create a repeating To-do**

1. Create a To-do and set **Repeat** before saving. You can’t add a repeat rule later.
2. Choose **Every day**, **week**, **month**, **year**, or **Custom**.
3. For a custom schedule, set the interval, weekdays, day of the month, or a pattern such as **the second Tuesday**. Set when the series ends.
4. To edit or delete a recurring todo, choose **This occurrence**, **This and future**, or **Entire series**.
5. Choose **Complete forever** to end the series today. To show every occurrence, go to **Settings > To-do > Show every repeat**.

## **Know the repeat rules**

1. A recurring To-do can’t be moved or duplicated directly.
2. If a monthly To-do is set for the 31st, it falls on the last day of shorter months and continues on the last day afterward.
3. Past occurrences can’t be edited.

## **Set a reminder**

1. Add a due date and time to the todo.
2. Choose **At time of due** or set a custom time before it. A reminder can’t be set for after the due time.
3. For recurring To-dos, choose **At due**, **At start**, or **10 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, or **3 hours before**.
