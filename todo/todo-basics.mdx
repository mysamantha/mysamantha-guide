---
title: "To-do basics"
description: "Use To-do to capture tasks, add details, and organize them into lists."
---

## **Find your to-dos**

1. **Inbox:** Review To-dos that haven’t been organized into a list.
2. **Today:** See today’s To-dos. Overdue To-dos appear in their own group, with **Reschedule to today**.
3. **Upcoming:** See To-dos due in the next 30 days.
4. **Filters:** View Upcoming, Completed, Cancelled, Overdue, Recurring, or Archived To-dos.
5. Use the due-date badge to check when a To-do is due. MySamantha remembers your last-used view.

<Note>
  In a workspace, the **To-do** tab opens **Projects**.
</Note>

## **Add a To-do**

1. Enter a title. You can include dates, times, priorities (`p1` to `p4`), repeat patterns, date ranges, or a list using `#list`.
2. Check the **Date**, **Priority**, **Reminders**, **Repeat**, and **List** chips before saving.
3. You can also add To-dos by voice and review them before they’re created, or ask Samantha in chat.
4. After adding a To-do, use **Undo** to reverse it or **Open** to view it. 

## **Manage a todo**

1. Add a description, list, section, start date, due date, priority, reminder, repeat rule, or status.
2. Add subtodos with **Add sub-todo**, or drag one todo onto another.
3. Completing or cancelling can be undone. Archiving is reversible; deleting is permanent.

## **Organize lists and sections**

1. Create a list with **New list**, then give it a name and color.
2. Add sections to organize a list. Sections can be edited, moved, duplicated, or deleted.
3. Deleting a list or section also deletes its To-dos. The Inbox can’t be renamed, deleted, or divided into sections.

## **Choose a view**

1. Use **List**, **Board**, or **Calendar**. Calendar has **Month** and **Week** views, plus a **No date** tray. Phones show List view only.
2. Group by Date, Priority, or Section. Sort by Date, Priority, or Name. Filter by priority.
3. Use **Show completed** or **Reset view** to change the display. Search with`/`, including archived To-dos.
4. Shift-click to select multiple To-dos, then reschedule, move, set priority, archive, or delete them. View settings are saved in your browser.
