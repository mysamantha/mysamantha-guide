Excluded Labels lets you control which parts of your inbox Samantha should skip. This is useful if you want Samantha to focus on important conversations and ignore lower-priority email categories like newsletters, promotions, or automated updates.

## Why use Excluded Labels

Not every email label needs attention. By excluding labels, you can reduce noise and keep Samantha focused on the emails that are actually relevant to your work.

## What happens when a label is excluded

When you exclude a label:

- Emails with that label are ignored in relevant email workflows
- Summaries stay more focused
- Inbox triage becomes cleaner

## Common labels to exclude

You may want to exclude labels like:

- Promotions
- Social
- Newsletters
- Automated notifications
- Low-priority updates

## How to update excluded labels

1. Open **Settings**
2. Select **Excluded Labels**
3. Choose the labels you want Samantha to ignore
4. Save your changes

## Tips

- Only exclude labels you rarely need to review
- Avoid excluding labels used for important client or team communication
- Review your excluded labels from time to time as your workflow changes
